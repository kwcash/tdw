How it plays

Three actions. Then the cards land.

A turn takes a minute. An operation takes a handful of turns. The pressure builds whether you are ready or not.

Read the board

Twelve domains carry pressure, shields and slopes. Your opponent’s cards sit above them and name where they will land. Some arrive face down.

Spend three actions

Detect a hidden card. Disrupt a card you can see. Defend a domain with a shield. An idle action costs you coherence, and coherence buys actions.

Name the lever

One domain carries the whole operation. Name it right and leverage drops and your Disrupts there cost less. Name it wrong and the opponent gains.
