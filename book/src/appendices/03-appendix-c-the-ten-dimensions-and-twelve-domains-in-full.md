## Appendix C. The Ten Dimensions and Twelve Domains in Full

*The Physics and the Battlefields*

Appendix A gave the ten Dimensions their formal TOGAF treatment. A name, a statement, a rationale and the implications that follow. This appendix gives both catalogs, Dimensions and Domains, their full reference detail instead. The working metaphor, a documented Party application and a Western historical parallel come first, followed by the measure and the fix this book proposes for each.

None of the numbered estimates below are measurements. Each is this book's own analytical judgment, stated as such at first use here and everywhere it recurs. A reader who thinks the true ratio runs at half the stated figure still faces the same argument, because nothing in this book turns on the exact number.

### The Ten Dimensions

**1. Time.** The size of the board, not the timing of a single blow. The Party plans in five-year blocks toward 2049 and took two decades to dismantle Hong Kong's autonomy, one step too small each time to trigger a hard answer. \[1\] American power runs on an eighteen-month planning window between elections. The 2015 breach of the Office of Personnel Management, which took the security files of 21.5 million people, drew no lasting penalty across three administrations. \[2\] Congress has reached for a longer horizon before, at Bretton Woods and at Los Alamos, and let it collapse back within days of each crisis passing. **Measure and fix.** The Party holds roughly a sevenfold edge in working horizon. The fix is structural. Competition strategy written into law for a decade or more, and accounts an annual budget cannot raid.

**2. Entropy.** How much force a system loses to friction on the way from decision to result. The Party locked down Wuhan, eleven million people, inside a day. \[3\] The fentanyl trade, run through a Chinese-to-cartel supply chain, killed 48,422 Americans in 2024. That is down from 76,282 the year before, while the supplier keeps a full layer of deniability. \[4\] Eighteen American intelligence organizations arguing with one another is the mirror image, a system that cannot move at the speed of one order. \[5\] The Civil War proves entropy is nobody's national trait. Confederate currency inflated to a fraction of face value inside three years, for want of a workable tax system, while the Union held one stable currency for the duration under the same pressure. **Measure and fix.** The Party moves roughly five times faster. The fix is not central control. One binding assessment a year that every agency answers to, plus real enforcement power in one body across all sixteen infrastructure sectors, would close the gap instead.

**3. Topology.** The shape power flows through, star or mesh. The Party runs a star. One hub reaches every province, state firm, university and citizen in a single hop. The physical version runs through Belt and Road. The influence version runs through more than six hundred United Front-linked overseas groups. \[6\] The West is a mesh by design, resilient to any single failure and slow to coordinate because every node answers to no one above it. Lincoln built the American demonstration first, running the telegraph office himself to collapse a chain of command that had taken weeks into an exchange that took hours. **Measure and fix.** The star coordinates roughly two and a half times faster. The fix is not to become a star. A thin layer of star-speed coordination grafted onto the mesh, for the single job of strategic assessment alone, answers better. Back it up wherever the hubs an adversary would target first happen to sit.

**4. Asymmetry.** Fighting where you are strong against where the rival is weak, and the edges compound rather than add. The Party stacks at least five. An open society against a closed one. Years of Western process against days of Party action. Cheap living-off-the-land intrusion against expensive total defense. An open society's visibility against a closed one's opacity. The attacker's choice of time and place against a defender who must guard everywhere. \[7\] Scott's naval blockade in 1861 and Mao's 1938 doctrine of concentrating against weakness while declining the enemy's chosen ground are the same refusal, arrived at independently, seventy-six years and one ocean apart. \[8\] **Measure and fix.** Stacked, the illustrative ceiling runs past ten thousand to one, a shape rather than a quantity. The fix is reciprocity as a rule. Price every asymmetric access, and tie the access a Chinese entity gets in an American sector to the access an American entity gets in return.

**5. Agency.** Whether a target was ever free to choose, beneath both Gradient and Resonance. The Party has captured real ground inside the institutions Americans trust most. Roughly a quarter of surveyed China scholars report self-censoring, and no order need ever be given once tuition and grant money have done the work. \[9\] Lend-Lease is the American demonstration in reverse, dependence bought rather than captured, arming allies with material they could not repay and watching their options narrow without a single directive. **Measure and fix.** The Party's pull runs roughly twelve to one against near-zero access flowing the other way. The fix is disclosure. Real-time public reporting of foreign funding above a set line for universities, think tanks and media, and cooling-off periods of a decade or more for officials moving into China-linked work.

**6. Resonance.** Whether every domain points one way, so effort multiplies instead of cancelling. Eleven of twelve domains aligned against Taiwan, from trade dependence to the collapse of its diplomatic allies to a message of inevitable defeat, so the twelfth domain lands with the weight of the rest. \[10\] The Gettysburg Address did the same work for the Union in two hundred seventy-two words, a message pitched at a frequency the audience was already tuned to receive. **Measure and fix.** The Party runs close to ninety-five percent domain alignment against a Western thirty. The fix is one coordinating authority with the standing to stop a Defense designation and a Treasury listing from pointing opposite ways.

**7. Phase.** Whether the blows land together or arrive in a sequence the target can answer one at a time. Hong Kong's National Security Law took effect at a single hour on June 30, 2020. The arrests, the frozen accounts, the pulled apps and the dismissals landed together. No window opened to regroup. \[11\] Grant's simultaneous 1864 offensives and the five Normandy beaches fixed to one dawn prove the same principle needs no exotic technology, only the refusal to run a plan as a sequence when running it at once is available. **Measure and fix.** The Party's synchronization runs near eighty-five percent against a Western forty. The fix is pre-authorization. Design and approve the response before the trigger, so the deliberation happens before the event rather than during it.

**8. Gradient.** The slope that moves behavior with no order given. Access to a market of 1.4 billion buyers, revocable overnight, makes compliance the easy path. A studio rewrites a film before Beijing asks, and the deniability is total because nothing was ordered. \[12\] The Liberty Loan drives of 1917 and 1918 cut the same incline in the other direction, making bond purchase the socially visible default until purchase rates climbed past what any tax could have compelled. **Fix.** Flatten the slope rather than chase the order that was never given. Build second sources in the most exposed sectors, and end the post-government payday with real cooling-off periods.

**9. Criticality.** The tipping point where a small push starts a fall far larger than the push. The Party's near-monopoly on rare-earth processing forced a Japanese climbdown within weeks in 2010 after a single administrative halt, and Taiwan's concentration of advanced chip fabrication makes a single strait the hinge of the global economy. \[13\] The Allied bombing campaign against German oil in 1944 crippled the Wehrmacht's fuel within months. An earlier campaign against ball-bearing plants destroyed real capacity but produced no comparable collapse, because production dispersed and substitutes appeared faster than the campaign could close every avenue. **Fix.** A critical chokepoint yields roughly a tenfold multiplier. Map the West's own chokepoints first, stockpile and second-source the worst of them, and hold whatever chokepoints the West already occupies inside the rival's own supply chains as deterrents rather than trading them away for short-term profit.

**10. Coherence.** The master Dimension, deciding whether the other nine add or cancel. Democratic centralism and a personnel system that controls every appointment from governors to editors gave the Party's Zero-COVID policy total reversal, nationwide, in seventy-two hours. \[14\] Pearl Harbor produced the American demonstration without any command structure forcing it. The same alignment built the arsenal and, in the same years, authorized the internment the Supreme Court upheld in *Korematsu* and Congress later redressed. \[15\] Neither precedent, a shock or a purge machine, is a model worth wanting. **Measure and fix.** The Party runs close to ninety-five percent institutional alignment against a Western twenty-five. The compound edge across all ten Dimensions runs, illustratively, near eight hundred to one. The fix comes first, before any other repair, because every other investment gets partly cancelled without it. A standing council built to convene rather than command.

### The Accelerant: Why AI Is Not an Eleventh Dimension

Artificial intelligence does not join the ten Dimensions as an eleventh. It removes the human friction that once rationed all ten at once. Coordinating one stratagem across three domains used to take a bureaucracy that never tired and a network that never leaked, a constraint of patience rather than secrecy. AI erases the constraint. It does not select a favorite Dimension. It lifts the ceiling on Time, Entropy, Phase and every other term at once. Millions of simulations run against a model of a target's economy and politics faster than the target can finish observing the first move.

The Party was first to the engine because it already held every precondition. A single chain of command with almost no internal friction left for the engine to remove, a population it could model without consent, and no institutional check that would object on principle to turning the whole apparatus on a civilian population. \[16\] China is the proof of concept, not the patent holder, and the disposition the engine converts into capability is not a nationality. A posture toward the world travels to any actor, state or otherwise, that reaches the same threshold of will and access.

The engine cuts both ways for whoever holds it. A system coherent enough to act as one and fast enough to act before anyone can object has built a machine for executing mistakes flawlessly as well as strategies. The Party's own policy whiplashes are a preview. The durable answer is not a faster machine. A society coherent and clear-eyed enough that an injected signal does not shatter it, when the machine that injects it arrives in more than one capital, is what actually lasts.

### The Twelve Domains

**1. Kinetic.** The domain every reader already understands, and the one where non-use does the work. The People's Liberation Army's missile inventory and near-daily incursions into Taiwan's air defense identification zone shape Taipei's politics without a shot fired, each incursion banked as evidence for the next warning. \[17\] The German battleship *Tirpitz* tied down a disproportionate share of the Royal Navy for three years while firing at almost nothing, sunk only in November 1944. A fleet in being is a fleet employed. **Fix.** Price the standing threat honestly, invest in mine countermeasures and distributed basing that lower a first strike's payoff, and stop scoring restraint as proof the threat was empty.

**2. Economic.** The flow of goods a country needs to live and fight, turned into leverage. The Party processes the large majority of the world's rare earths and, in 2023, restricted gallium and germanium exports to remind the West the valve exists. \[18\] Washington froze Japan's oil supply in 1941 after Japan occupied southern Indochina, holding the chokepoint the United States then controlled. Chokepoint control is proven American doctrine, not a Chinese invention. **Fix.** Lower criticality with stockpiles and funded second sources, treating the added cost as an insurance premium rather than a market failure.

**3. Financial.** Money and the rails that carry it. The variable interest entity routes American retirement money into flagged firms through a Caribbean shell, while one arm of government names the same firm a security risk and another keeps it listed. \[19\] Executive Order 8389 in 1940 and the freeze on Japanese assets in July 1941 prove the freeze is an American wartime instrument with its own pedigree. **Fix.** Reconcile the security-designation lists with the investment indices so one government stops funding what another forbids.

**4. Information.** The fight over a shared set of facts. Coordinated inauthentic networks push synthetic consensus in identical phrasing across supposedly independent accounts inside fifteen-minute windows. \[20\] The Office of War Information proved in 1942 that a democracy could run coordinated messaging at national scale without shutting its own free press, the same instrument the Party now runs without that restraint. **Fix.** Treat the recommendation algorithm as the weapon it is, subject to security review and independent audit, not only the data it runs on.

**5. Psychological.** The war on the will to keep resisting. Transnational repression holds a relative hostage to a diaspora member's silence abroad, an operation this book's *Long Arm* documents in full. \[21\] SHAEF's Psychological Warfare Division proved in 1944 and 1945 that leaflets and radio, aimed at the will rather than the equipment, could produce tens of thousands of surrenders combat alone would have cost far more to achieve. **Fix.** Name transnational repression a national-security offense rather than a private dispute, and protect the diaspora as its target rather than treating it as a suspect population.

**6. Legal.** Law written as a weapon and worn as a shield. The Anti-Secession Law of 2005 pre-licensed force against Taiwan years in advance, so the Party can call any future move lawful from its first hour. \[22\] The Foreign Agents Registration Act of 1938 ran the opposite instinct first, exposing an actor already in the room rather than licensing an action in advance. **Fix.** Build pre-authorized fast tracks for review in genuinely strategic cases, so the law can move in weeks without weakening the ordinary process everyone else relies on.

**7. Association.** Coalitions bought and coalitions fractured. The United Front Work Department runs a star of influence through hundreds of overseas organizations, and Belt and Road debt buys the silent abstention of the borrower at bodies like the UN Human Rights Council. \[23\] The Atlantic Charter and the Declaration by United Nations built a twenty-six-nation coalition on shared purpose in 1941 and 1942. The Party's version buys the same alignment with debt instead. **Fix.** Compete with better terms in the most contested states, and expose front groups so a borrowed voice stops passing for a native one.

**8. Cyber.** The fight inside the networks everything else runs on, decided by pre-positioning rather than theft. Volt Typhoon's implants lived inside American water, power and telecommunications systems for months using the target's own tools, taking nothing and waiting for the hour a Taiwan crisis would need them to fail together. \[24\] The Special Operations Executive and the Office of Strategic Services ran the identical architecture with human cells across occupied France, activated on a single coded broadcast the night before D-Day. **Fix.** Hunt for the implant on the assumption it is already there, and speed the handoffs between the agencies and the private owners who hold the infrastructure.

**9. Cultural.** What a society admires and teaches, moved without an order. Market access to one of the largest box offices on earth gets studios to censor their own scripts before Beijing has to ask. \[25\] The Office of War Information's Bureau of Motion Pictures ran a milder, four-year version of the identical leverage, trading favorable film-stock allocations for cooperative scripts. **Fix.** Require disclosure of any editorial condition tied to foreign-market access, and reduce dependence on the single market that makes the slope so steep.

**10. AI and Cognitive.** The race to see, decide and act first, compressed toward zero. The Party's Digital Twin, a high-fidelity population model built from harvested data, aims every other domain. Its sharpest synthetic media are procedural forgeries, not sensational fakes. \[26\] Colossus at Bletchley Park proved the principle in 1944, reading enciphered German orders before their own recipients had finished acting on them. **Fix.** Keep a human at every high-consequence node, and slow the automation pipeline just enough at decisive points to let judgment intervene.

**11. Biological.** Degradation kept below the threshold of acknowledged war. Chinese-sourced fentanyl precursors, cooked by Mexican cartels, killed 48,422 Americans in 2024. That is a real decline from 76,282 the year before, and it reflects enforcement rather than any change in supply. \[27\] Malaria hospitalized more Marines on Guadalcanal in 1942 than combat wounds did, proving a domain does not need an enemy attack to prove itself decisive. **Fix.** Attack the proxy chain at the broker and the precursor rather than policing only the street, and answer the trade as statecraft rather than ordinary crime.

**12. Data, Surveillance and Intelligence.** Knowing more about the target than the target knows about you, at population scale and at committee scale together. The Ministry of State Security runs collection abroad and policing at home as one function, while the National Security Act of 1947 walls the CIA out of the FBI's own domestic lane. \[28\] The Census Bureau's supply of block-level population data used against Japanese Americans in 1942 is this domain's darkest American precedent, and Operation Overlord's fusion of Ultra decryption with the Fortitude deception is its clearest demonstration done right. **Fix.** Stand up a genomic-data authority with review standing equal to a port or a chip plant, and build one small fusion cell cleared to trade leads across the wall before a case is already filed.
