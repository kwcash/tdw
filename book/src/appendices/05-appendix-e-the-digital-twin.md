## Appendix E. The Digital Twin

*A Working Model of Everything the Party Means to Aim*

**DOMAINS** Economic · Financial · Information · Psychological · Legal · Association · Cyber · AI and Cognitive · Biological · **Data and Surveillance (spine)**

**DIMENSIONS** Time · Entropy · Topology · Asymmetry · Agency · Resonance · Phase · Gradient · **Criticality (signature)** · **Coherence (master)**

**STRATAGEMS** Spine S9 · S12 · S13 · S20 · S35

**GAPS** Six, one at each layer. Signature gap at the Data layer, where the defenders' own data is for sale.

In September 2020 researchers published a leaked database from Zhenhua Data, a company in Shenzhen that described its clients as including the Party's military. The database held profiles of about 2.4 million people around the world. The profiles covered politicians and officers, executives and academics, and their families. Zhenhua assembled them largely from open sources and sorted people into categories such as politically exposed persons and their relatives and close associates. Analysts found that about a fifth of the data did not come from open sources. Assessments of the database's value conflict, from an aspirational commercial venture to a real tool of influence. \[1\]

The harvest behind such files is older and larger. In 2015 the Office of Personnel Management lost the background-investigation records of about 21.5 million Americans, including the forms that list a cleared person's relatives, debts and foreign contacts. The health insurer Anthem lost records on about 78.8 million people that year. Equifax lost the financial records of about 147 million Americans in 2017. The Justice Department indicted four officers of the People's Liberation Army for the Equifax breach in 2020. It charged a Chinese national in the Anthem breach in 2019. \[2\] \[3\]

In November 2024 the FBI and the Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency reported that Party-linked hackers had broken into multiple American telecommunications carriers. They stole call records and reached the systems carriers use to fulfill court-ordered wiretaps. Investigators named the operation Salt Typhoon. \[4\]

The Party keeps files on its own people too. It has kept a personnel dossier on every urban worker, student and official since the 1950s. In Xinjiang a police platform flags people for travel, prayer and phone use and sends officers to investigate. \[5\]

This case calls the assembly a Digital Twin. The twin is a working model of people and companies and of the assets, minerals and supply chains they depend on. It also models spectrum and psychology. It runs across time and learns from events. The term is the framework's own. The record documents every harvest named here. No public document shows the Party running one unified twin. The assembly is the framework's argument.

### The Terms of This Case

A digital twin, in engineering, is a live software model of a physical system. Sensors feed it, and engineers use it to forecast failure and test changes before touching the real machine. The architecture carries over to a population. Data feeds the model, and the model forecasts behavior and tests pressure before anyone applies it.

The actor is the Party. The Chinese people are the twin's first and most completely modeled population. Their dossiers, their health codes and their real-name registrations built the method before it faced outward.

The case keeps one line that must survive any edit. It never treats a person's ancestry or nationality as evidence. It describes a method of collection and modeling. The defense it proposes models dependencies, never people.

### 1. Architecture Vision

**Documented.** The State Council's plan for artificial intelligence of July 2017 set the goal of world leadership in the field by 2030. The National Intelligence Law obliges every organization on the mainland to support state intelligence work. The Party's rules on human genetic resources, in force since July 2019, restrict the export of Chinese genetic data. \[6\] \[7\]

**Argued.** The aim is a model complete enough to aim every other domain. Such a model shows whom the Party can recruit, pressure or ignore. It marks the firm, mineral or cable whose loss would cause the most harm, and the division in a society that carries the most charge.

### 2. The Party's Baseline

#### Business Architecture. The Capabilities It Fielded

The Data and Surveillance domain carries the operation, because the twin is the capability that lets every other capability aim.

**Economic.** The Party models supply chains well enough to govern them abroad. On October 9, 2025 its Ministry of Commerce claimed export-control jurisdiction over foreign-made products containing as little as 0.1 percent Chinese-origin rare-earth value. The rule copied America's foreign direct product rule and added a 50 percent affiliates rule. The Party suspended it on November 7, 2025 for one year, in exchange for the American suspension of Commerce's affiliates rule. Controls on gallium and germanium date from August 2023. \[8\]

**Financial.** The capital flows in *The Borrowed Knife* feed the model with the ownership and funding of foreign firms.

**Information.** The published record of the open world feeds the model directly. The Patent and Trademark Office publishes new applications every Thursday and offers the full corpus as bulk data. \[9\]

**Psychological.** The recommendation data in *The Cognitive-Industrial Complex* map a society's moods and divisions.

**Legal.** The intelligence law makes every mainland company a potential sensor. The genetic-resources rules keep Chinese data at home while foreign data flows in.

**Association.** Talent programs and united-front networks add who knows whom.

**Cyber.** Intrusion supplies what the open record does not. The OPM, Anthem, Equifax and Salt Typhoon breaches each added a layer.

**AI and Cognitive.** Machine learning turns the harvest into forecasts at a speed no human staff could match.

**Biological.** BGI, the Party-favored genomics firm, sold a prenatal test used by millions of women in dozens of countries. Reuters reported in 2021 that BGI stored genetic data from the test in a national gene bank and developed the test with the PLA. The National Counterintelligence and Security Center warned in February 2021 that the Party was collecting American health and genomic data. Commerce added BGI units to the Entity List in 2023. \[10\]

**Data and Surveillance.** The domain assembles the rest.

#### Data Architecture. The Twin's Schema

The model spans time. The dossiers run back to the 1950s, and the stolen background forms of 2015 describe people who will still hold clearances in the 2040s. A background form lists relatives and debts, which change slowly. Stolen once, it stays useful for a career.

**Documented.** American military doctrine for psychological operations runs target audience analysis. The analysis rates a group's vulnerabilities, its susceptibility to a message and the accessibility of channels that reach it. \[11\]

**Argued.** A twin of this breadth can run the same analysis on a whole population. It can score vulnerability, susceptibility and accessibility person by person, and keep the result as a dossier. The *Psychological Operations* case takes up that use.

#### Application Architecture. The Discovery Loop

The twin's routine is a loop. It gathers and models, then forecasts and acts, then measures and retrains.

**Documented.** Human Rights Watch reverse-engineered the app behind Xinjiang's Integrated Joint Operations Platform in 2019. The platform collects data on phone use, travel, electricity use and religious practice. It flags people whose patterns it deems suspicious and sends officers to investigate, and the officers' reports feed back into the platform. \[5\]

**Argued.** The same loop runs outward. The patent corpus arrives weekly and machine reading can process it the same day. A model can tell which foreign inventions have no mainland counterpart and which inventors to recruit. It can see which suppliers to watch. Each probe, each recruitment and each export-control announcement becomes an experiment whose result retrains the model. Checking forecasts against events is what turns a database into a twin.

**Stratagem 13, Stomp the Grass to Scare the Snake**, probes to see how the target responds and records the response.

**Stratagem 12, Take the Opportunity to Pilfer a Goat**, takes every record a breach exposes whether it helps now or later.

**Stratagem 9, Watch the Fire from Across the River**, lets the open world publish and reads.

**Stratagem 20, Muddy the Waters to Catch the Fish**, uses the model to pick the divisions worth stirring.

**Stratagem 35, Chain the Stratagems**, runs the loop across every domain. The record documents each harvest and the domestic loop. The outward loop is the framework's reading.

#### Technology Architecture. The Platform It Ran On

The twin runs on the mainland's compute, its cloud and its platforms, and on the management plane of *Military-Civil Fusion*. Its most useful foreign platforms are the defenders' own. In Salt Typhoon the intruders reached the systems American carriers built for court-ordered wiretaps. The access point built for lawful use served whoever held it. \[4\]

#### Principles and Governance. How It Held Together

**Criticality.** The twin exists to find chokepoints, the node where a small push causes a large fall.

**Coherence.** One authority can join what its harvest collects. The record documents the harvests. The joining is the framework's argument.

### 3. The Defenders' Baseline

No single enterprise defended the defenders' data. Each breach met its own owner.

#### Business Architecture. Who Owned What

| Layer | Defender owner | Instrument |
|---|---|---|
| Federal personnel records | Office of Personnel Management | Its own security, breached in 2015 |
| Health and credit records | Private firms, under sector regulators | Breach notification and fines |
| Commercial data brokers | Federal Trade Commission | A 2024 law barring sales to foreign adversaries |
| Bulk sensitive data | Justice Department | A 2025 rule on transfers to countries of concern |
| Telecommunications | Carriers, the FCC and CISA | Carrier security and advisories |
| Genomic data and biotech | Commerce, and federal procurement | Entity List and the 2026 procurement bar |
| Critical minerals | Geological Survey, Energy and Defense | Lists of critical minerals |

#### Data Architecture. What the Defenders Knew

The defenders' own data stayed on sale for years. Commercial brokers bought and sold Americans' location, health and financial data with few limits. Congress barred brokers from selling Americans' sensitive data to foreign adversaries in April 2024. The Justice Department's rule on bulk data took effect in April 2025. It restricts transfers of genomic and biometric data to countries of concern, along with health, location and financial data. \[12\]

The flow of genetic data ran one way. The Party restricted the export of Chinese genetic data from 2019. American genetic data reached mainland firms until the controls of 2023 and later. \[7\] \[10\]

#### Application Architecture. The Routines the Defenders Ran

The defenders treated each breach as an incident. OPM, Anthem, Equifax and the carriers each ran their own response. Prosecutors indicted hackers, and regulators fined firms. Treasury sanctioned a mainland company in January 2025 for its role in Salt Typhoon. \[3\] \[4\]

The defenders ran no discovery loop across the harvests. No public office assessed what the combined breaches let an adversary model.

#### Technology Architecture. The Platforms the Defenders Used

The defenders built lawful access into their own networks. The carriers' wiretap systems exist because the law requires them. In Salt Typhoon the adversary used them.

#### Principles and Governance. What Held the Defenders Together

The defenders kept their principles. A free society does not keep a dossier on every citizen, and it should not. That principle is the reason the target twins things and never people.

### 4. Gap Analysis

**The principles audit**

| Principle | The Party's design | The defenders' design |
|---|---|---|
| **Time** | Keeps what it takes for decades | Treats each breach as a closed incident |
| **Entropy** | One authority can join every harvest | Each breach has its own owner |
| **Topology** | A hub that reads every layer | A mesh of firms and agencies |
| **Asymmetry** | Bars export of its genetic data | Let its own flow out for years |
| **Agency** | Every mainland firm is a potential sensor | Brokers sold data to anyone until 2024 |
| **Resonance** | Harvests feed one model | Harvests are handled one at a time |
| **Phase** | Reads the patent corpus weekly | Wrote data-transfer rules in 2024 and 2025 |
| **Gradient** | Collection rides on commerce and convenience | Data sales earned revenue with no security price |
| **Criticality** | Models chokepoints well enough to write a 0.1 percent rule | Lists critical minerals without a model of the flows |
| **Coherence** | Governed by one authority | No owner of the aggregate exposure |

The cells summarize documented acts. The audit itself is the framework's reading.

#### Gap 1. No Owner of the Aggregate Exposure

*Architecture Governance*

**Documented.** OPM, Anthem, Equifax and the carriers each handled their own breach. Prosecutors and regulators acted case by case. \[2\] \[3\] \[4\]

**Argued.** Each breach was an incident to its owner and a layer to the adversary. No one assessed what the layers add up to once joined.

#### Gap 2. The Harvest Is Permanent

*Architecture Principles, Time*

**Documented.** The stolen background forms of 2015 describe people who still hold or seek clearances. \[2\]

**Argued.** Data that changes slowly stays useful for decades. The defenders close an incident in months, and the adversary's model keeps the record for a career.

#### Gap 3. The Data Layer Was for Sale

*Data Architecture. The signature gap*

**Documented.** Brokers sold Americans' sensitive data with few limits until the 2024 law. The Justice Department's bulk-data rule took effect in 2025. The Party restricted the export of its own genetic data from 2019. \[7\] \[12\]

**Argued.** The adversary did not need to steal what it could buy. The defenders' data architecture had a market where it needed a control, and the controls arrived years after the Party closed its own door.

#### Gap 4. Dependencies Without a Model

*Business Architecture*

**Documented.** The Party wrote a rule that traces Chinese content through foreign products at a threshold of 0.1 percent. The defenders keep lists of critical minerals. \[8\] \[13\]

**Argued.** A list names what matters. A twin shows how it flows, where it chokes and what fails when it stops. The Party showed it holds the second, and the defenders mostly hold the first.

#### Gap 5. No Discovery Loop

*Application Architecture*

**Documented.** The Party runs a gather, flag, act and feedback loop at home. The defenders' analysis runs by agency and by request. \[5\]

**Argued.** The side with the faster loop learns faster from every contact. The defenders publish their inventions weekly and read their own corpus slower than the adversary reads it.

#### Gap 6. The Lawful Door Opened for the Wrong Hand

*Technology Architecture*

**Documented.** Salt Typhoon reached the wiretap systems American carriers built for court orders. \[4\]

**Argued.** An access point built into a platform serves whoever holds it. The defenders built a door for their own courts, and the adversary walked through it.

### 5. Target Architecture

The target rests on one principle. Twin things, not people. The Party models its citizens and foreigners. The defenders should model minerals and supply chains, spectrum and infrastructure, and their own exposure. They should never keep a dossier on their citizens.

**Gap to target**

| Gap | Target element | Owner | What it costs |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. No owner of the aggregate exposure | A standing assessment of what all known breaches let an adversary model, joined across owners | National Strategic Competition Council, with the intelligence agencies | Classified effort, and uncomfortable findings |
| 2. The harvest is permanent | Security practices that assume stolen data stays live, such as rotating what can be rotated for exposed people | OPM and the clearance agencies | Cost, and burden on cleared personnel |
| 3. The data layer was for sale | Minimum collection and firm limits on broker sales, extended to allies | Congress, the FTC and the Justice Department | Revenue lost by brokers, and friction for research |
| 4. Dependencies without a model | A dependency twin of minerals, drugs and components that shows flows and chokepoints | Commerce, Energy, Defense and Health, with allies | Data from private firms, and protection of their secrets |
| 5. No discovery loop | An allied loop that reads the open corpus, including patents, as fast as any adversary | Research agencies and the Patent and Trademark Office | Compute, and analysts |
| 6. The lawful door opened | Wiretap systems built to security standards, with the access points audited | Congress, the FCC and the carriers | Cost to carriers, and debate over lawful access |

**Drugs belong in the dependency twin.** The Drug Enforcement Administration names mainland suppliers as the main source of fentanyl precursor chemicals, and the Justice Department charged eight mainland chemical companies in October 2023. American generic medicines depend on foreign active ingredients whose exact origins the Food and Drug Administration has told Congress it cannot fully trace. A dependency twin shows both flows without modeling a single person. \[14\]

**The twin of things protects liberty.** A dependency model needs data about shipments, factories and ores. It needs no data about any citizen's beliefs, and the target keeps it that way.

**What the target would not do.** It would not match the Party's twin. A free society that built a dossier on every citizen would become the thing it defends against. That claim remains an argument.

### What the Digital Twin Proves

It proves Criticality. The twin exists to find the node where a small push causes a large fall, and the Party showed in October 2025 that it can see supply chains well enough to govern them abroad.

It proves the dossier is old and only the scale is new. The personnel files of the 1950s, the health codes of 2022 and the stolen background forms of 2015 serve the same purpose. The Chinese people were the first file.

It proves the defenders' weakness sits in their own data architecture. They sold what the adversary would otherwise have had to steal. They built doors the adversary used, and they published their inventions faster than they read them.

The answer is not a Western twin of the Western citizen. The answer is a twin of things. It covers the ores and pills, the chips and cables and the flows a free society depends on, and someone who looks at the whole owns it.

This appendix has sketched the architecture in outline. The twin itself deserves the fuller treatment a single case chapter cannot give it. Its data model, its discovery loop and the gap it leaves open all belong to work beyond this book's scope. What stays fixed regardless of how far that future work goes is the one line this appendix opened with. Twin things, not people.
