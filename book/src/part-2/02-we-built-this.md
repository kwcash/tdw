## We Built This

*The Country That Ran the Method Best Stopped Running It*

The strongest evidence in this book is not Chinese. It comes from what the United States did between 1941 and 1945, and from what the United States did to itself in the decades after.

### The Arsenal

Read domain by domain, the American war effort is total domain war in its purest expression, and a reader is entitled to ask why a method the country once ran at the largest scale in history now reads as foreign.

**Economic.** The War Production Board converted civilian industry wholesale. Automobile plants stopped making cars in February 1942 and made tanks, aircraft and trucks instead. In April 1944, Ford's Willow Run plant produced 453 aircraft in 468 hours, one finished B-24 every sixty-three minutes. The plant accounted for roughly half of all B-24s assembled that year. \[1\] American yards drove Liberty ship construction from roughly 355 days early in the program to about 42 days by 1943, against the ten to twelve months an oceangoing ship had required in 1917. \[2\] By 1944 American munitions output exceeded that of the Axis powers combined. \[3\] This was the deliberate conversion of an economy into an arsenal, directed from the center, not the market operating on its own.

**Scientific.** Vannevar Bush directed the Office of Scientific Research and Development, which fused universities, industry and the military into one research instrument. \[4\] Radar, the proximity fuze and mass-produced penicillin each changed the war. The Manhattan Project sits at the extreme end. Peak employment reached 130,000 and total spending reached \$2.2 billion, but turnover means the true figure runs closer to half a million Americans who worked on some part of the project, near one percent of the wartime civilian labor force. \[5\] No single domain built the bomb. Physics and industry, security and logistics, and command fused into one operation.

**Financial.** War bonds pulled savings out of the population and into the war effort, absorbing purchasing power that would otherwise have driven inflation. At Bretton Woods in July 1944, before the war was won, the United States wrote the financial architecture of the world that would follow it. \[6\] This domain was not merely funding a war already underway. The same dollars were building the peace still to come.

**Information, intelligence and coalition.** The Office of War Information shaped the domestic narrative, and Frank Capra's *Why We Fight* turned the machinery of entertainment into the machinery of morale. \[7\] The Office of Strategic Services fused espionage and sabotage with analysis and unconventional warfare in one organization. \[8\] Codebreaking gave commanders the enemy's intentions in near real time. The Grand Alliance held three powers with irreconcilable long-term aims together across Tehran, Yalta and a continuous machinery of conferences, with Lend-Lease arming allies as an instrument of strategy in its own right. \[9\]

Domains are where the war was fought. Coherence is why it compounded. Pearl Harbor did in a single morning what years of argument could not. It aligned a fractious, regionally divided democracy into near-total unity, and for four years the country ran at a level of coherence it has never reached before or since. \[10\] The reversal matters more than the achievement. America did not win by staying the decentralized system it had always been. For the duration it became a centrally coordinated system, then chose to change back once the winning stopped. \[11\]

### The Enclosure

A pen is not a trap. A trap springs shut. A pen is built.

The men who take wild hogs in Texas do not chase them. Chasing scatters a sounder across a hundred square miles and teaches every animal in it to fear the open ground. The professionals build a corral instead, in ground the hogs already cross. They leave the gate wide and put out corn. The animals come. Nothing happens. They come back. Nothing happens again. Over some weeks the enclosure stops reading as a structure and becomes a feature of the landscape, no more remarkable than a creek bed. Then a man closes the gate, and it takes about a second.

**Argued.** The animals inside were not stupid. They reasoned correctly the entire time from the evidence available to them. Corn appeared and nothing bad followed. The panels were always there and never did anything. The conclusion drawn from a hundred safe visits was the wrong conclusion, not because the reasoning failed but because the relevant fact never entered the frame.

America built itself a pen in the decades after the coherence described above, six panels, each raised for a reason that was defensible at the time.

**The money power.** The capacity to create money moved decisively toward the financial sector and away from the productive one, a reversal within living memory. \[12\] New money reaches finance first.

**The multiplication.** The Federal Register ran 2,620 pages in 1936 and 80,756 pages in 2022. \[13\] Congress has passed roughly ninety-two thousand rules since 1996 and repealed sixteen. Each rule answered a real failure. The aggregate answers nothing.

**The license.** Occupational licensing covered about five percent of American workers in the 1950s and covers roughly a quarter now. \[14\] Part of the rise is mechanical, tracking the shift from making goods to providing services. The rest is not mechanical, and the measured effect includes barriers to entry that incumbents write and enforce.

**The credential.** A private accreditor, answerable to no electorate, stands between a person and a public function. The Department of Veterans Affairs requires a social worker at a Vet Center to hold a master's degree from a program accredited by one specific body, and a doctorate does not satisfy the requirement. \[15\] That provision cannot be a competence rule. It functions as a credential rule instead, and no elected official reviews what the credentialer requires.

**The curriculum.** Congress passed Goldwater-Nichols in 1986 for one reason. The armed services had specialized past the point of being able to fight together. \[16\] The law fixed jointness among the services. It left the boundary between the military and everything else exactly where it found it.

**The lane.** Work that crosses disciplinary boundaries carries a measurably lower probability of funding. A study of 18,476 proposals submitted to the Australian Research Council's Discovery Programme over five years found the penalty consistent, falling hardest on the young researcher least able to absorb it. \[17\] Nobody forbids the crossing. The slope simply runs the other way.

**Argued.** No adversary built any of these six panels. Most were built by people trying to protect the public, and many succeeded at exactly what they were built to do. This book does not manufacture a conspiracy where none exists. Nobody convened, and no document ordered the result. Several million people hold the pen in place today, each making a locally rational decision inside incentives none of them designed. Most would defend their own panel sincerely, and correctly on its own narrow terms.

That is the harder problem, not the softer one. A plot can be exposed, and exposure ends it. An accumulation has no author to expose, no meeting to reveal and no leak to publish.

### The Country Once Named This

One document shows the forgetting was not total, or not immediate.

**Documented.** George Kennan directed the State Department's Policy Planning Staff. On May 4, 1948, he sent the National Security Council a memorandum titled "The Inauguration of Organized Political Warfare." \[18\] He wrote that political warfare is "the employment of all the means at a nation's command, short of war, to achieve its national objectives." \[18\] He listed six means in one breath. Political alliances and economic measures such as the Marshall Plan. Open propaganda and clandestine support for friendly foreign movements. Covert psychological operations and encouragement of resistance inside hostile states.

**Argued.** Kennan was not describing a future adversary's playbook. He was naming an instrument for his own government to build, three years after the arsenal above had already proven the country could run every domain at once. The memorandum shows that the whole-of-government habit did not vanish the moment the shooting stopped in 1945. It survived at least until 1948, in the mind of the diplomat who had just finished explaining Soviet behavior to Washington, before the six panels described above had fully closed.

What Kennan asked for in 1948 and what the country actually built afterward are not the same thing, and the gap between the request and the result is this chapter's real subject. The men who ran the arsenal knew how to fuse every domain into one instrument. The man who asked to keep doing it on purpose, in peacetime, got a fraction of what he asked for. The rest of what he asked for is what the chapters ahead find missing.
