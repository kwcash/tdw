## Taiwan: The Republic of China

*The One Campaign That Runs Every Domain, Every Dimension, and Every Stratagem at Once*

Between October 1 and October 4, 2021, the air force of the People's Liberation Army flew 149 sorties into the Republic of China's air-defense identification zone. The count set a record at the time. \[1\] October 1 is the founding anniversary of the People's Republic, and the Party chose the date. The flights crossed no legal threshold and drew no military response. The following week the planes flew again.

Since then the Party has fitted a named exercise to each political event it wished to answer. It launched Joint Sword-2024A three days after President Lai Ching-te took office in May 2024. Joint Sword-2024B came four days after his National Day speech that October. Strait Thunder-2025A followed his speech of March 13, 2025. Justice Mission-2025 began on December 29, twelve days after Washington notified Congress of an $11 billion arms package. \[6\] \[18\]

The defenders run a different clock. The first new F-16 Block 70 for Taiwan, sold in 2019, left the assembly line in March 2025 about two years late. Guided bombs notified to Congress in 2017 now look set to arrive in 2027 or 2028. \[18\]

The Party answers in days, and the defenders deliver in years. The record documents both clocks. Reading them as one architecture against another is the framework's argument.

Taiwan is the one place where all twelve domains, all ten dimensions and nearly the whole stratagem catalog run at once against one target. The campaign runs in the open, and its openness belongs to the design.

### The Terms of This Case

Taiwan is the Republic of China, a free and self-governing Chinese society of roughly twenty-three million people. It has its own elected government, military and currency. The People's Republic has never governed it for a single day. The Party calls its aim reunification, but no one can reunify what was never one polity under the regime now claiming it. What the Party proposes is annexation.

The aggressor is the Chinese Communist Party, never China and never the Chinese people. The Party has ruled the mainland since 1949 and holds its 1.4 billion people under its authority. The People's Liberation Army proves the distinction. Its soldiers swear loyalty to the Party, and the Party's own doctrine gives it absolute leadership over the armed forces. \[2\]

The ideology driving the campaign is not Chinese tradition. Marx and Engels wrote the doctrine in nineteenth-century Europe, and Lenin turned it into a method of total state control in Russia. The Party runs a local franchise of a European import. The campaign against Taiwan heals no ancient wound. A Leninist movement seeks to extinguish the most successful free Chinese society in history.

That explains why the Party cannot negotiate the campaign away. The Republic of China threatens the Party's legitimacy, not its territory. The Party claims the Chinese people need it for order and dignity. Taiwan refutes that claim every day it governs itself. The war is one of regime survival dressed as national restoration.

### 1. Architecture Vision

**Documented.** The Anti-Secession Law of 2005 authorizes non-peaceful means if Taiwan declares independence, if a major incident threatens unification, or if the Party judges the possibilities for peaceful unification exhausted. The Party alone decides when any trigger has fired. Its congress report of 2022 called complete reunification an essential requirement of national rejuvenation. \[4\]

**Argued.** The aim runs wider than conquest. The Party seeks absorption without a war it might lose, by making resistance look irrational and absorption inevitable. Every domain serves that one judgment in the minds of twenty-three million people.

### 2. The Party's Baseline

#### Business Architecture. The Capabilities It Fielded

All twelve domains carry weight in this case, and every one points at the same judgment.

**Kinetic.** The Party fights below the threshold that would justify a kinetic answer. The PLA flew more than a thousand incursions into Taiwan's identification zone in 2023 alone. \[5\] Named exercises now follow named political events within days. The Party's coast guard joined them for the first time in May 2024. \[6\]

**Economic.** The Economic Cooperation Framework Agreement of 2010 bound the two economies together. \[3\] The Party used it as a lever in the open. It suspended tariff concessions on 12 products three weeks before Taiwan's January 2024 election, and on 134 more eleven days after President Lai took office. \[7\]

**Financial.** Taiwanese firms with mainland operations depend on approvals and capital the Party controls. The Party can raise the cost of capital for anyone whose politics it wishes to discipline, and it needs no headline to do it.

**Information.** The information operation aims less at winning affection than at teaching futility. Aligned media and coordinated networks carry one message into Taiwan's own press, that absorption is coming and only its manner remains open.

**Psychological.** Three frequencies run together. Defeatism says Taiwan cannot win and America will not come. Identity pressure recasts self-government as cultural betrayal. Cost amplification turns every exercise into a preview of the price.

**Legal.** The Anti-Secession Law is the Party's self-issued license, and its triggers are elastic by design. \[4\] The One China framing the Party presses on governments and companies worldwide carries the same instrument abroad. The Party also recasts United Nations Resolution 2758 as authority for a claim the resolution never makes. \[8\]

**Association.** The United Front Work Department cultivates business leaders and local officials inside Taiwan, along with temple associations and media figures. Accommodation then arrives in Taiwanese voices. In June 2025 Taiwan's prosecutors charged four former staff members of the governing party with spying for the mainland, including former aides to the president's office and a former foreign minister. A district court convicted and sentenced all four that September, with terms from four to ten years. On appeal in June 2026 the High Court reduced three of the sentences and acquitted the fourth outright. \[9\]

**Cyber.** Taiwan's government networks absorb continuous intrusion and pre-positioning. The logic matches the Volt Typhoon operation documented against American infrastructure. \[10\]

**Cultural.** Subsidized tourism, temple ties, youth exchanges and entertainment access deepen a shared identity the Party can then convert into a political claim.

**AI and Cognitive.** Around the January 2024 presidential election, Taiwan faced a documented surge of deepfaked audio and video, fabricated documents and amplified narratives. \[11\]

**Biological.** The Party uses sanitary pretexts as trade weapons. It banned Taiwanese pineapples in March 2021 and grouper in June 2022, each at a moment of political friction. \[12\]

**Data and Surveillance.** Platform data, intrusion and the Party's wider collection assemble a picture of Taiwan's leaders. The picture covers their networks, their finances and their vulnerabilities.

#### Data Architecture. What It Knew

The data layer is again the least documented part of the Party's baseline. The precision of the timing shows it at work. Each exercise names its trigger, each tariff suspension lands on a sector and a date, and each agricultural ban falls on a constituency the Party wants to teach.

That precision argues for a working model of Taiwan's politics, this case's Digital Twin. No public document describes it.

**Stratagem 4, Wait at Ease for the Weary Enemy**, sets the patience. The Party lets Taipei and Washington tire across electoral cycles.

**Stratagem 19, Remove the Firewood from Under the Pot**, strips the foundations one ally at a time. Taiwan's formal allies fell from about thirty in the 1970s to twelve. \[13\]

**Stratagem 22, Shut the Door to Catch the Thief**, rehearses the blockade. Every named exercise since 2024 has practiced encirclement. \[6\]

**Stratagem 23, Befriend a Distant State While Attacking a Neighbor**, courts distant powers so the near target stands alone.

**Stratagem 3, Kill with a Borrowed Knife**, lets Taiwan's own mainland-dependent businesses and cultivated media carry the argument.

**Stratagem 1, Deceive the Heavens to Cross the Sea**, turns the extraordinary into routine. Daily incursions hide real preparation inside the familiar.

**Stratagem 33, Sow Discord Among the Enemy**, amplifies Taiwan's divisions and the friction between Taipei and Washington.

**Stratagem 35, Chain the Stratagems**, links the rest. An election brings a tariff suspension, an inauguration brings an exercise, and each move feeds the message of inevitability.

Stratagem 36 is **If All Else Fails, Retreat.** Each exercise ends short of a red line, which preserves the option for the next cycle.

The record shows each routine. Reading them as one chain is the framework's argument.

#### A Target Audience Note

The Taiwanese electorate of January 2024 fits this form, and the case carries this book's fullest illustration of the discipline because every other case builds toward it.

**Documented.** Around the January 2024 presidential election, Taiwan faced a documented surge of deepfaked audio and video, fabricated documents and amplified narratives. \[11\] The Party timed a tariff suspension on twelve products to land twenty-three days before the vote. \[7\]

Voters with mainland business ties carry the highest accessibility, reached directly through the same commercial channels that carry ordinary trade. When a state must police a name, the enmity belongs to the state and not to the nation. The 1.4 billion people of the mainland are the campaign's subjects, as the people of Taiwan are its targets.

#### Gap 1. No Owner of the Clock

*Architecture Governance*

**Documented.** The State Department approves arms sales and Congress receives the notifications. The Defense Security Cooperation Agency and industry execute them, and Taiwan's legislature funds them. The backlog stood at about $32 billion in January 2026. Guided bombs notified in 2017 slipped to 2027 or 2028, and the first new F-16 ran about two years late. \[15\] \[18\]

**Argued.** Each owner behaved correctly inside its own remit. Nobody owned the time between a notification and a delivery. The Party's exercise has one owner and a clock measured in days. The defenders' answer has five owners and a clock measured in years.

#### Gap 2. The Party Already Runs the Target Architecture

*Architecture Principles, Phase. The signature gap*

**Documented.** The trigger table records six answers timed to six events. The Party's statements or its state media named the political trigger for each exercise. For the tariff suspensions it cited Taiwan's trade restrictions, and the timing is the record's own. Every answer landed within twenty-three days of its trigger, and one landed before the election it aimed at. \[6\] \[7\]

**Argued.** A named trigger, a package built in advance and a published justification make up the architecture the Hong Kong case asks the defenders to build. The Party runs it already, against Taiwan, in public. No defender instrument in the public record fires on any of these triggers. The defenders need not share the Party's aims to copy its timing.

#### Gap 3. A List Only One Owner Reads

*Data Architecture*

**Documented.** Defense designated Hesai a Chinese military company in January 2024, a year after its Nasdaq listing. A federal court upheld the designation in July 2025. The designation's statutory consequence reaches Defense contracting. Three senators asked the SEC and Treasury in September 2025 to delist the company, and it remained listed in 2026. \[16\]

**Argued.** The designation lives in one owner's records and binds one owner's contracts. Capital markets never read it. The defect sits in the schema, which has one reader where the campaign needs four.

#### Gap 4. The Routine Reaches the Captain

*Application Architecture*

**Documented.** Taiwan's court convicted the captain of the Hong Tai 58 and sentenced him to three years. An appeal failed, and a later ruling awarded the cable's owner about $570,000 in damages. Prosecutors cited the ship's changing names and flags. They charged the captain alone and sent the rest of the crew home. The two cables to the Matsu Islands cut in February 2023 still have no formal attribution. \[19\] \[20\]

**Argued.** Criminal law reaches the person on the deck. It stops at the owner, the registry and whoever sent the ship. The Party builds its gray-zone acts to land in that space.

#### Gap 5. Taiwan Absorbs Coercion Alone

*Business Architecture, Economic*

**Documented.** The Party suspended tariff concessions twice around political events in 2023 and 2024. It banned pineapples in 2021 and grouper in 2022. \[7\] \[12\]

**Argued.** Each ban lands on a named Taiwanese constituency. Taipei answers each one with its own support and its own search for buyers. No allied instrument in the public record has answered any of these bans, so the Gradient runs downhill toward accommodation.

#### Gap 6. The Leading Edge Sits on One Island

*Technology Architecture*

**Documented.** TSMC makes the overwhelming majority of the world's most advanced logic chips on Taiwan. Its first Arizona plant began volume production in late 2024. \[17\]

**Argued.** The concentration gives Taiwan a shield and gives the world a single point of failure. Diversification has begun and remains small beside the island's capacity.

**The shield has a price.** Building leading-edge capacity abroad lowers the world's exposure and lowers Taiwan's leverage in the same motion. The defenders owe Taiwan that value back in another currency, in arms delivered on time and trade that does not bend under coercion.

**What the target would not have done.** It would not stop the incursions or the cultivation. It would shorten the defenders' clock and make each coercive act cost the Party something. That claim remains an argument.

### What Taiwan Proves

Taiwan proves that every layer of the framework can run at once against one target. All twelve domains, all ten dimensions and nearly the whole catalog appear in the record.

It proves something none of the other cases shows as clearly. The Party already runs the architecture the defenders lack. It names a trigger, fires a package built in advance and publishes the justification, and it does so in days.

It proves the Party manufactures the enmity. The Party wages the campaign in the name of a people who are not driving it. National restoration is the costume. A war of regime survival against a free, prosperous and self-governing Chinese society is the body underneath.

The Party conducts the campaign in the open because it believes no adversary is coherent enough to stop it. That belief, not the island and not the strait, is the actual target.

### Field Card

**What happened.** Every domain, dimension and nearly the whole stratagem catalog ran against one target at once. The Party answered a president's inauguration with a military exercise within days and a trade suspension within weeks, while American arms Taiwan had already paid for sat on a production line for years. A named trigger, a package built in advance and a published justification arrived on schedule six times in two years. It happened in public, because the Party believes no coalition of defenders is coherent enough to stop it.

**The gap.** The Party already runs the architecture the defenders lack, the signature gap at the Principles layer, and no single owner holds the clock between an American arms notification and a Taiwanese delivery.

**The fix.** A published delivery schedule across the whole arms pipeline, paired with packages the defenders agree on in advance for named gray-zone triggers, would let the defenders answer in the same days the Party already uses.

**Where you could work on it.** Foreign area officer or China-desk work inside State or Defense, the arms sales and defense production pipeline, or semiconductor and supply-chain policy aimed at the concentration this case's final gap describes.
