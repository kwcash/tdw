## Military-Civil Fusion

*The Long Reach That Conscripts Every Company, Researcher, and Citizen*

**DOMAINS** Economic · Financial · Information · Psychological · **Legal (spine)** · Association · Cyber · Cultural · AI and Cognitive · Biological · Data and Surveillance

**DIMENSIONS** Time · Entropy · Topology · **Asymmetry (signature)** · Agency · Resonance · Phase · Gradient · Criticality · **Coherence (signature)**

**STRATAGEMS** Spine S3 · S8 · S14 · S17 · S24 · S31 · S35

**GAPS** Six, one at each layer. Signature gap at the Governance layer, where the Party runs a management plane and the defenders run unconnected lists.

In October 1944, in the Vosges Mountains of eastern France, the 442nd Regimental Combat Team fought for six days to reach a Texas battalion cut off in the forest. The 442nd was a unit of Japanese Americans, and many of its men had volunteered from internment camps where their own government held their families. It took more casualties than the number of men it rescued. It ended the war as the most decorated unit of its size and length of service in the history of the United States Army. \[2\]

They could prove their loyalty because Imperial Japan could not reach them. The claim concerns capability, not restraint. To coerce a Japanese American in 1942, Tokyo would have needed a current map linking him to relatives in Japan. It would have needed a channel across a severed Pacific, a way to verify his compliance and a law obliging him to obey. It had none of them. The government's own Munson and Ringle reports had already found the population overwhelmingly loyal. \[3\]

Run the thought experiment forward. Give Imperial Japan a statute obliging every citizen to assist its intelligence organs on demand. Give it a registry of emigrants' relatives, instant encrypted channels into any American city and the means to punish a mother for her son's refusal. The Nisei's loyalty would not change. His position would, because a hostile state would hold a lever on him that he did not ask for and could not refuse.

The Chinese Communist Party holds that lever today. The variable that changed since 1942 is not the character of the people. The regime's reach changed. Military-Civil Fusion is the deliberate construction of that reach.

The experiment's hardest result stands in every section that follows. Even where the reach is real, internment would have been the wrong answer, because it punishes the person who carries the lever. The answer is to cut the lever and shield the people.

### The Terms of This Case

**Ancestry is not loyalty.** The internment of Japanese Americans was a moral catastrophe and a strategic error. The threat from Military-Civil Fusion is never a person's Chinese ancestry. Any defense that drifts toward suspecting Chinese students, researchers or Chinese Americans as a class repeats the internment error and does the Party's work for it.

The actor throughout is the Party. Sun Yat-sen, who founded the Chinese republic, studied in Honolulu at the school now called Punahou and drew on Lincoln for his Three Principles of the People. \[4\] The Party is not his heir. The Party runs a local franchise of a European ideology, and its rule produced the deadliest famine in recorded history and the Cultural Revolution. \[5\]

The Chinese people are Military-Civil Fusion's first conscripts. A graduate student in an American laboratory, legally bound to surrender what she learns if the state demands it, is far more often the Party's conscript than its agent.

### 1. Architecture Vision

**Documented.** The National Security Law of 2015 made safeguarding national security a duty of every citizen and organization. The National Intelligence Law of 2017 obliges every organization and citizen to support, assist and cooperate with state intelligence work. A central commission for integrated military and civilian development, chaired by Xi Jinping, dates from January 2017. \[1\] \[6\]

**Argued.** The aim is to erase the line between the civilian economy and the People's Liberation Army. Any advance in a laboratory, company or university on the mainland then becomes available to the army, and any access the open world grants becomes a channel home.

### 2. The Party's Baseline

#### Business Architecture. The Capabilities It Fielded

The Legal domain carried the operation, because the statute supplies the compulsion that every other channel rests on.

**Economic.** Civilian hardware from Party-controlled manufacturers entered foreign critical systems through ordinary procurement. A state-owned rail maker whose parent sits on the Defense list won American transit contracts on price. Telecommunications equipment from Huawei and ZTE ran in rural American networks. \[7\] \[15\]

**Financial.** Firms tied to military modernization raised money in Western markets and sat in the index funds that hold retirement savings. *The Borrowed Knife* carries that record.

**Information.** Western laboratories publish, and the fusion system reads.

**Psychological.** The family at home is the lever. A researcher abroad knows that his parents live within the regime's reach, and the knowledge needs no threat to work.

**Legal.** The statutes are the master key. No purely private company or apolitical researcher exists in the Party's law where its security interest is concerned. A firm on the mainland cannot credibly promise a foreign partner that its technology will stay civilian, because the promise is not the firm's to make. \[1\]

**Association.** Talent programs, led by the Thousand Talents Plan, drew established researchers with funding and prestige. The United Front Work Department cultivates overseas communities and professional associations. \[8\]

**Cyber.** Intrusion takes whatever the legal and financial channels cannot reach.

**Cultural.** Universities on the mainland carry the fusion into education. The seven universities known as the Seven Sons of National Defence answer to the industry ministry that oversees defense production. \[9\]

**AI and Cognitive.** Commercial champions route algorithms and talent toward the army's pursuit of what it calls intelligentized warfare.

**Biological.** Genomics firms and the military medical academies gather genetic data and research at global scale.

**Data and Surveillance.** Data-sharing mandates fuse commercial information into the security apparatus.

#### Data Architecture. What It Knew

The Party knows which of its own firms serve the army, because it certifies them. A private firm that wants to make weapons needs four certificates. They cover the firm's quality system, its secrecy qualification, its research and production license and its standing as an equipment supplier. Firms list the certificates in their brochures and annual reports. \[10\]

The Party's registry of its own military supply chain therefore exists, and much of it sits in public filings. Its model of the targets abroad is the least documented part of its baseline, and the precision of the talent recruitment argues that one exists.

#### Application Architecture. The Routines It Ran

**Stratagem 24, Borrow the Road to Conquer Guo**, is the signature. Civilian collaboration and market access are the road, and military capability travels down it.

**Stratagem 3, Kill with a Borrowed Knife**, lets foreign universities and companies fund and perform the research before the results go home.

Stratagem 8 is **Openly Repair the Gallery Roads, March Secretly Through Chencang.** It runs an openly civilian enterprise whose output reaches the army.

**Stratagem 14, Borrow a Corpse to Resurrect the Soul**, repurposes civilian research and dual-use products for military ends.

**Stratagem 17, Cast a Brick to Attract Jade**, offers modest grants and positions to extract research worth many times their cost.

**Stratagem 31, The Beauty Trap**, cultivates the key people whose cooperation brings a laboratory's knowledge with them.

**Stratagem 35, Chain the Stratagems**, runs the channels as one engine. Investment opens the door, talent walks through it, and intrusion takes the rest. The record shows each channel. The chain is the framework's reading.

#### A Target Audience Note

Military-Civil Fusion's talent programs fit this form, and the researcher with family still reachable by Chinese law serves as the example.

**Documented.** The Thousand Talents Plan and successor programs recruited researchers with funding, laboratory space and prestige. \[8\] The National Intelligence Law binds any citizen or organization within reach of Chinese state authority, family members included, whether or not that person has ever worked for the state.

**Argued.** A target audience analysis would read the audience narrowly, as researchers and their immediate family who hold Chinese citizenship or residency and therefore fall within the law's reach, never as an ethnic or ancestral category. A Chinese American with no citizenship, no residency and no family on the mainland sits outside this audience entirely, whatever his name or appearance. Vulnerability runs through family location, not blood. Susceptibility runs highest where a researcher's parents, siblings or children live on the mainland and the researcher has not yet been asked for anything. The line rarely needs to be a threat, because the researcher can calculate the exposure himself. Accessibility runs through the Thousand Talents Plan's own recruiters, through professional associations the United Front Work Department cultivates, and through routine visits home that carry no risk until the law decides otherwise.

#### Principles and Governance. How It Held Together

**Asymmetry.** Western laboratories, markets and universities stay open by design. The Party's equivalents do not reciprocate, and its law obliges its entities to carry home what they find.

**Coherence.** The Party abolished by law the line between civilian and military that the West's whole protective architecture assumes. The record documents the statutes and the institutions. The single design behind them is the framework's argument.

### 3. The Management Plane and the Banality of the Machine

In a computer network the data plane carries the traffic, and a separate management plane configures every device. An administrator on the management plane can change any router's rules without touching the traffic itself. The Party built its state the same way.

**Documented.** Every unit of the People's Liberation Army from regiment up carries a political commissar beside its commander, and every company carries a political instructor. The Party requires state enterprises to write the Party committee's role into their articles of association. The securities regulator's governance code of 2018 requires listed companies to set up Party organizations. \[11\]

The plane reaches further into private firms. The Party's Organization Department claimed Party units in 73 percent of private firms in 2017. The Party-led business federation's own survey found 48 percent in 2018. More than 92 percent of the 500 largest private firms host Party units, and so did about 106,000 foreign-invested firms by the Party's count for 2016. \[12\] In the largest internet firms the state also holds special management shares with board rights, as *The Borrowed Knife* records.

**Documented.** A Party directive of 2014 found that the four weapons certificates connected poorly, with cumbersome reviews and long approval cycles. It ordered the systems linked and the cycles shortened. In 2015 the catalog of weapons items that required a license shrank by 62 percent. \[10\]

**Argued.** Read as an architecture, the commissar and the Party committee form an out-of-band management plane with administrator rights on every node that matters. Military-Civil Fusion is the routing policy pushed through that plane. The National Intelligence Law is the root credential that every node must accept. The 2014 directive is the Party running a systems-integration project on its own bureaucracy.

Mao Zedong stated the principle in 1938, in the middle of a war for survival. "Our principle is that the Party commands the gun, and the gun must never be allowed to command the Party," he wrote. \[18\] The commissar beside every PLA commander from regiment up is that principle staffed, not merely stated.

The machine runs on forms. Hannah Arendt called the thing she saw in Jerusalem in 1961 the banality of evil, the ordinary clerk doing ordinary work inside an extraordinary system. \[13\] Military-Civil Fusion needs no conspiracy in any single office. It needs certificates issued on schedule, study sessions held in the committee room and approval cycles kept short. A clerk who shortens a review cycle for a weapons license is doing his job well. The strength of the system is that almost no one inside it has to decide anything grave.

The defenders run on bureaucracy too. Their clerks keep lists, and each list is correct. The difference is architectural. The Party's clerks sit on one management plane, and the defenders' clerks sit in separate offices with no plane above them.

### 4. The Defenders' Baseline

No single enterprise defended the open world's laboratories and networks. Each list had its own owner.

#### Business Architecture. Who Owned What

**Lists against owners**

| List or control | Owner | What it does | Year |
|---|---|---|---|
| Entity List and Military End-User List | Commerce Department | Requires licenses for exports | Entity List from 1997 |
| Section 1260H list | Department of Defense | Bars Defense contracting from 2026 | 2021 |
| Non-SDN Chinese Military-Industrial Complex list | Treasury Department | Bars Americans from buying listed securities | 2021 |

Laid over the capability map, the lists show seven owners, seven legal tests and seven clocks.

#### Data Architecture. What the Defenders Knew

The defenders' identity model counts ownership. Commerce's affiliates rule took effect on September 29, 2025. It extended Entity List restrictions to any firm at least 50 percent owned by a listed entity. The rule counts ownership and not control. \[14\]

#### Application Architecture. The Routines the Defenders Ran

Each list runs its own designation routine, and each routine answers in court on its own terms. In March 2021 a federal judge blocked the Defense designation of Xiaomi, and Defense agreed to remove it that May. The investment ban then moved to Treasury's own list with a new test. \[16\]

The fixes arrive one product line at a time. Congress barred federal transit funds for listed rail makers in December 2019, after contracts had already gone to one. \[7\] The FCC ordered carriers to rip out Huawei and ZTE equipment. Congress funded $1.9 billion of the cost in December 2020 and the $3.08 billion shortfall in December 2024. \[15\] Commerce suspended the affiliates rule on November 10, 2025 as a concession in a trade agreement, six weeks after it took effect. The suspension runs until November 9, 2026. \[14\]

#### Technology Architecture. The Platforms the Defenders Used

The defenders' networks ran the adversary's hardware. Rural carriers built on Huawei and ZTE equipment because it was cheap, and subway systems bought rail cars on price.

#### Principles and Governance. What Held the Defenders Together

The defenders kept their principles. Universities stayed open to the world's students, and courts made designations prove their case. Those principles are the thing worth defending. The defenders had no plane above their lists and no policy that decided what one designation should trigger in another office.

#### Gap 1. Lists Without a Policy Engine

*Architecture Governance. The signature gap*

**Documented.** Seven owners keep seven lists with seven legal tests. Defense's list bars contracting, Treasury's bars investment and Commerce's governs exports. Congress's defense act for fiscal 2026 ordered a report on harmonizing the sanctions lists. \[16\]

**Argued.** In IT terms the defenders run access-control lists without a policy engine. Each list is correct, and no policy decides what a designation in one office should trigger in the others. The Party runs one management plane that configures every node.

#### Gap 2. Fixes Take Years and Can Be Traded

*Architecture Principles, Phase*

**Documented.** The FCC's replacement program waited four years for full funding. The transit ban came after an award. The affiliates rule lasted six weeks before a trade agreement suspended it. \[7\] \[14\] \[15\]

**Argued.** A control that a trade negotiator can suspend becomes a bargaining chip. The Party's acquisition cycle runs continuously, and the defenders' controls run on appropriations and negotiations.

#### Gap 3. The Defenders Count Ownership, the Party Holds Control

*Data Architecture*

**Documented.** The affiliates rule counts ownership at 50 percent. The Party controls firms through Party units, special management shares of about one percent and the four certificates. \[10\] \[12\] \[14\]

**Argued.** The defenders' identity model looks for the owner, and the Party's control runs through the management plane. A firm can sit outside every ownership test and still answer to a Party committee. The four certificates are a public signal of military supply that no list reads by default.

#### Gap 4. The Laboratory Decides Alone

*Business Architecture*

**Documented.** NSPM-33 of January 2021 required research institutions to run security programs and require disclosure. The State Department revoked more than a thousand student visas under Proclamation 10043 in 2020. A federal jury convicted the Harvard chemist Charles Lieber in December 2021 of lying about his ties to the Thousand Talents Plan. \[8\] \[17\]

**Argued.** Each university runs its own screen against a coordinated acquisition system. The one screen the defenders run centrally sorts people by institutional affiliation. The Lieber case shows the screen must target undisclosed funding, not ancestry. The screening capability has no owner above the individual laboratory.

#### Gap 5. Designation Routines Built One Statute at a Time

*Application Architecture*

**Documented.** A federal judge blocked the Xiaomi designation in 2021, and Defense withdrew it. The investment ban moved to a new list with a new test. \[16\]

**Argued.** Each list grew from its own statute and answers in court on its own terms. The weakest routine sets the pace for the rest, and the adversary tests each one separately.

#### Gap 6. The Hardware in the Walls

*Technology Architecture*

**Documented.** Huawei and ZTE equipment ran in rural American networks until the FCC ordered it out. A rail maker whose parent sits on the Defense list won American transit contracts on price. \[7\] \[15\]

**Argued.** Civil procurement had no security function, so the adversary's hardware entered the defenders' platforms by the cheapest bid. Congress then fixed it one category at a time.

### 6. Target Architecture

The target cuts the lever and shields the people. It keeps universities open and never screens anyone by ancestry.

**Gap to target**

| Gap | Target element | Owner | What it costs |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Lists without a policy engine | One designation schema, with a published matrix of what each designation triggers in every other office | National Strategic Competition Council, convening only | A statute that outlives an election cycle, and agencies that give up their separate tests |
| 2. Fixes take years and can be traded | Replacement programs funded in the same act that orders them, and security controls kept out of trade concessions | Congress and the Trade Representative | Budget up front, and less room to bargain |
| 3. The defenders count ownership | An identity test that counts control, including Party units, special management shares and the four certificates | Commerce and Treasury | Heavier due diligence, and harder evidence standards |
| 4. The laboratory decides alone | A shared screening service for research funding and direction, available to every university | National Science Foundation and research agencies | Money, and friction with academic freedom |
| 5. Designation routines built one statute at a time | Published criteria and an appeal route common to every list | The council's registry | More litigation up front |
| 6. The hardware in the walls | A procurement security screen by category rather than by product | Federal procurement and grant agencies | Higher prices, and fewer bidders |

**The guardrail governs every row.** Each element aims at the Party's legal and institutional reach. None aims at Chinese students or Chinese Americans as a class, because suspicion by ancestry hands the Party the outcome it wants.

**The affiliates rule is due back.** Commerce's rule returns on November 10, 2026 unless the government acts again. The target asks that its return count control as well as ownership.

**What the target would not have done.** It would not have closed the open world's laboratories, and it would not have stopped the Party's statutes. It would have given the defenders one plane above their lists. That claim remains an argument.

### What Military-Civil Fusion Proves

Military-Civil Fusion proves Asymmetry. A free society's openness is its attack surface, and a regime that fused its civilian and military sectors by law can convert that openness into capability faster than the target can screen it.

It proves the architecture of the banal. The Party's machine runs on commissars and committees, on certificates and short approval cycles. Almost no one inside it decides anything grave. The defenders run on lists kept by conscientious clerks in separate offices. Both run on bureaucracy. Only one has a plane above it.

It proves, most of all, whom the reach conscripts first. A law none of them voted for binds the researcher, the founder and the student abroad. In 1942 the United States interned a population against which no foreign lever existed. Today the lever exists, and the Party wrote it into law. The answer does not change. Cut the lever. Shield the people.

### Field Card

**What happened.** A 2015 law made every Chinese citizen and organization legally bound to assist state intelligence work on demand, with no exceptions for private firms or individual researchers. That law converted millions of ordinary commercial and academic relationships into potential channels for military technology transfer, whether or not any individual ever intended to serve the army. Seven separate American agencies built seven separate lists in response, each testing ownership rather than control, each running on its own legal clock.

**The gap.** No American office holds a policy engine above the seven lists, the signature gap at the Governance layer.

**The fix.** A single designation schema that publishes what each list triggers in every other office would replace seven uncoordinated tests with one.

**Where you could work on it.** Export control and sanctions policy, university research-security compliance, or a career at one of the seven listing agencies pushing toward the harmonized schema this chapter argues for.
