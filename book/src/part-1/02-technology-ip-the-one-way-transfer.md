## Technology IP: The One-Way Transfer

*Thirty Years of Innovation, Moving in One Direction*

**DOMAINS** Economic · Financial · Information · **Legal (spine)** · Association · Cyber · AI and Cognitive · Data and Surveillance

**DIMENSIONS** Time · Entropy · Topology · **Asymmetry (signature)** · Agency · Resonance · Phase · Gradient · Criticality · **Coherence (signature)**

**STRATAGEMS** Spine S7 · S9 · S12 · S18 · S28 · S35

**GAPS** Five, at five layers. Signature gap at the Data layer. The Technology layer holds the defenders' one working strength.

In 2016 a state-owned company in Fujian Province set out to build an advanced memory-chip plant with more than five billion dollars of public money. Fujian Jinhua Integrated Circuit had no memory technology of its own. It hired United Microelectronics Corporation of Taiwan to develop the process. \[1\]

UMC hired engineers from the Taiwan subsidiary of Micron Technology, the last major American maker of memory chips. Two of them had copied Micron files before they left. One used a Micron file to adjust UMC's design rules for the memory it was building. \[2\]

Then the instruments went to work, and each ran on its own clock. Taiwan's prosecutors raided UMC in February 2017 and indicted the company and three engineers that August. Micron sued UMC and Jinhua in California in December 2017. \[3\]

On January 12, 2018, UMC and Jinhua sued Micron's subsidiaries in Fuzhou. The court enjoined them from selling a list of Micron products on July 3. Micron said the court had ruled before hearing its defense. \[4\]

The American government moved that autumn. The Commerce Department placed Jinhua on the Entity List on October 29, 2018, which cut the plant off from the American tools it needed. Three days later the Justice Department unsealed an indictment of UMC, Jinhua and three engineers. The Attorney General launched the China Initiative the same day. \[5\] \[6\]

UMC pleaded guilty in October 2020 to receiving and possessing a stolen trade secret, and paid a $60 million fine. \[7\] A Taiwan court had convicted UMC and its engineers that June, and a retrial in 2022 cut the fine and acquitted one engineer. \[3\] On February 27, 2024, a federal judge acquitted Jinhua after a bench trial. \[8\]

The Party had its own answer for Micron. Its cyberspace regulator opened a security review of the company on March 31, 2023, and barred critical infrastructure operators from buying Micron chips fifty-one days later. In October 2025 Reuters reported that Micron would stop selling server chips to data centers on the mainland. \[9\]

Count the clocks. The Fuzhou court needed 172 days. The Party's regulator needed fifty-one. The Entity List stranded the plant at once. The criminal case ran more than five years and ended in acquittal. The record documents every date. Reading them as one architecture's failure is the framework's argument.

### The Terms of This Case

The case turns on the line between theft and diffusion. A society that studies published research, trains its own engineers and builds its own plants steals nothing. Japan did that after 1950 and South Korea after 1970.

Theft differs in kind. An employee carries a trade secret out under a nondisclosure agreement. A copier derives a design from a purchased part. An engineer uploads his employer's files to a personal account.

The record here needs the same care. UMC admitted receiving and possessing a Micron trade secret. The American court found the government had not proved its case against Jinhua. UMC's own account of the Taiwan ruling says that court found no evidence the secret reached Jinhua. No court has found that Micron's secret arrived at the Party's plant, and nothing here claims more than the courts found.

The actor throughout is the Party. Jinhua is a state-owned enterprise, and the plans that funded it are the Party's plans. The engineers who moved were Taiwanese employees taking better offers, a choice millions of engineers make every year. The public money came from the people of the mainland, and the Party asked none of them.

### 1. Architecture Vision

**Documented.** The Party published its aim. The State Council's science plan for 2006 to 2020 told industry to introduce foreign technology, digest it, absorb it and re-innovate on it. Made in China 2025, issued in May 2015, named integrated circuits among the sectors the country must master. The National Intelligence Law of 2017 obliges every organization and citizen to support state intelligence work. \[10\]

**Argued.** The operation reached further than any single plan. It set out to move the knowledge behind strategic technologies onto the mainland by every channel at once, and to pay less for it than the inventors paid. Seven channels served that one aim.

### 2. The Party's Baseline

#### Business Architecture. The Capabilities It Fielded

The Legal domain carried the operation, because law set the terms for every other channel.

**Economic.** Market access served as currency. The Trade Representative found in 2018 that the Party used joint-venture requirements and administrative approvals to pressure foreign firms into transferring technology. \[11\] Solar shows where the method ends. Bell Labs built the modern silicon cell in 1954, and by the early 2020s mainland manufacturers dominated every stage of the solar supply chain. \[12\]

**Financial.** State capital paid for acquisition at prices no private buyer would accept. Jinhua began with more than five billion dollars of public money. Variable interest entities let Party-favored firms raise Western capital in sectors the law closes to foreign owners. \[1\]

**Information.** Open research hands every reader the method at the moment of publication. The laboratory pays for the research, and the reader pays only for reading.

**Legal.** The legal channel ran both ways. Filing subsidies and quotas drove patent volume. The National Intellectual Property Administration conceded as much in January 2021, when it ordered filing subsidies ended by June and all patent subsidies phased out by 2025. \[13\] The Party's courts also served as counter-litigation. The Fuzhou court enjoined Micron within six months of a suit that UMC and Jinhua filed after Micron sued them in California. \[4\]

**Association.** Recruitment moved the knowledge that never publishes. UMC's engineers came from Micron's own staff. Talent programs offered senior researchers salaries and laboratories their home institutions could not match. \[14\]

**Cyber.** Intrusion takes what neither publishes nor walks out the door. In May 2014 the Justice Department indicted five officers of a People's Liberation Army unit for hacking American firms. The victims included SolarWorld, whose cost data and trade-case files the hackers took while SolarWorld was pursuing trade cases against mainland rivals. \[14\]

**AI and Cognitive.** A federal jury convicted former Google engineer Linwei Ding in January 2026 of stealing trade secrets on the chips and systems that train Google's AI models. He had taken more than two thousand pages while secretly affiliated with two companies on the mainland. The case produced the first American conviction for economic espionage involving AI. \[15\]

**Data and Surveillance.** The harvest runs through every published application and paper. Pat Choate reported to Congress's China commission in 2005 that a large mainland electronics maker mined foreign patent applications for its own product development. He also reported that Japanese officials logged seventeen thousand daily inquiries from the mainland against their own patent database. \[16\]

#### Data Architecture. What It Knew

The data layer is again the least documented part of the Party's baseline. The public record shows the harvest and its targets. It does not show the model.

The targets show that a map existed. Recruitment went to the engineers whose knowledge covered the process Jinhua lacked. The intrusion at SolarWorld went to the files on costs and trade litigation. Ding took the infrastructure behind frontier AI training, the layer that papers do not publish.

That map is this case's Digital Twin, a model of where Western technology sits and what moving it would cost. The precision of the targets argues for it, and no public document describes it.

#### Application Architecture. The Routines It Ran

**Stratagem 7, Create Something from Nothing**, stands up the structures that route Western capital into sectors the law closes to foreign owners.

**Stratagem 9, Watch the Fire from Across the River**, costs nothing. The open research community publishes, and the reader reads.

**Stratagem 12, Take the Opportunity to Pilfer a Goat**, takes whatever a partnership or a job exposes along the way.

**Stratagem 18, Capture the Ringleader to Disband the Bandits**, recruits the senior engineer whose files carry a process library.

Stratagem 28 is **Lure the Enemy onto the Roof, Then Remove the Ladder.** It offers a market a foreign firm comes to depend on. Micron built its first plant on the mainland in 2007, and the Party's regulator took the ladder away in 2023. \[9\]

**Stratagem 35, Chain the Stratagems**, runs the channels as one sequence. The published record shows what exists, recruitment reaches the people who know it, and intrusion takes the rest. The record shows each channel. The chain is the framework's reading.

#### A Target Audience Note

The recruitment channel fits this form. The engineer with process knowledge and no equity in keeping it serves as the example.

**Documented.** UMC hired engineers from Micron's Taiwan subsidiary who then moved files with them. Talent programs recruiting mainland and overseas researchers date to 2008 and offer salaries and laboratory funding beyond what many home institutions can match. \[14\]

**Argued.** A target audience analysis would read the audience as engineers and researchers holding process knowledge their current employer owns, not researchers as a class and never a national or ethnic category. Their vulnerability runs through career stall, pay compression against the cost of specialized skill, and a laboratory budget that says no more often than yes. Susceptibility runs highest where a researcher's expertise has plateaued in value at one employer and a competing lab offers to fund exactly the project the current one shelved. Accessibility runs through conferences, alumni networks and direct recruiter contact, the same channels any employer uses to hire. Funding and direction identify the channel. Ancestry identifies nothing, a distinction this case's own gap analysis makes explicit. Sun Tzu wrote that "knowledge of the enemy's dispositions can only be obtained from other men." \[19\] The recruited engineer is the oldest channel of all.

#### Technology Architecture. The Platforms It Ran On

The Party ran its routines on platforms it administers. Its courts heard the counter-suits. Its cyberspace regulator ran the security review under the Cybersecurity Law of 2016. Its patent office set the filing incentives, and its market decided which foreign firms could sell. Every American firm that sells or builds on the mainland operates on those platforms.

#### Principles and Governance. How It Held Together

**Asymmetry.** The American system discloses by design, and the Party's system absorbs by design. A published American application protects its inventor only where he files. It teaches every reader everywhere.

**Phase.** The Party's instruments landed in months. Its court needed 172 days and its regulator fifty-one.

**Coherence.** One set of published plans directed the capital, the courts, the patent office and the recruitment. The record documents the plans and each channel. That one authority coordinated them is the framework's argument, and it matches the Party's constitutional claim to lead every area of endeavor.

### 3. The Defenders' Baseline

No single enterprise defended the technology. Seven channels carried the transfer, and each met a different owner.

#### Business Architecture. Who Owned What

**Channels against owners**

| Channel | What the Party runs | Defender owner | Owner's instrument |
|---|---|---|---|
| Patent disclosure | Reads and files against published applications | Patent and Trademark Office | Examination, publication, and secrecy orders for single defense inventions |
| Trade secrets | Recruits the engineers who hold them | The firm, then the Justice Department | Civil suit, then prosecution |
| Market access | Trades access for technology | The firm, then the Trade Representative | Negotiation alone, then tariffs |
| Tools and chips | Buys what it cannot make | Commerce Department | Entity List and export controls |
| Capital | Raises money behind structures outsiders cannot see through | SEC and the audit board | Disclosure and inspection |
| Open research | Reads papers at publication | Universities and laboratories | Publication norms and research-security rules |
| Intrusion | Takes what neither publishes nor leaves | FBI and the firm's own security | Indictment and defense |

Laid over the capability map, the organization chart shows seven owners for one campaign, and none of them holds the whole.

**The firms.** Micron owned its secrets and fought for them. It sued in California, and its counsel took part in the Taiwan trial. It kept selling on the mainland. The firm decided alone what to risk. \[3\]

**The Trade Representative.** The office investigated forced transfer from August 2017 and imposed tariffs in 2018. Its four-year review in May 2024 found the Party had not eliminated many of the practices and had persisted rather than reformed. \[11\]

**The Commerce Department.** Commerce holds the export controls. Its Entity List stranded Jinhua, and its rules of October 2022 extended the controls to advanced chips and chipmaking tools across the mainland. \[5\]

**The Justice Department.** Justice holds prosecution. Its China Initiative began with the Jinhua indictment on November 1, 2018. The Department ended it on February 23, 2022. Critics had said it drifted toward academics charged with failing to disclose foreign ties. \[6\]

**The Patent and Trademark Office.** The office examines and publishes. Its only security screen, the secrecy order, applies to single inventions judged harmful to national security. \[17\]

**The capital regulators.** The SEC and the audit board hold disclosure and inspection. *The Borrowed Knife* carries that record in full.

#### Data Architecture. What the Defenders Knew

The defenders knew a great deal. Choate's 2005 paper for Congress's China commission described the patent harvest and recommended fixes. \[16\] The Trade Representative's 2018 report catalogued the transfer methods in detail. \[11\] In January 2021 the Patent and Trademark Office published its own study of the non-market factors behind mainland filing volume. The mainland's patent office ordered its subsidies cut two weeks later. \[13\]

Each finding lived in its owner's records. The public record shows no document that tied any of them to a response owned across the seven channels.

#### Application Architecture. The Routines the Defenders Ran

The Micron dispute ran through six instruments in three legal systems.

**The Micron clock**

| October 2018 | Commerce Department | Entity List designation of Jinhua \[5\] | The plant lost its American tools at once |

The instruments that needed no trial landed in months or at once. The American criminal case ran more than five years and lost against the Party's plant. The Taiwan case reached the engineers in Taiwan and ended in reduced sentences.

#### Technology Architecture. The Platforms the Defenders Used

The defenders' one working layer was technology. American firms make tools that every advanced chip plant needs, and the Entity List turned that position into a control that required no trial. \[5\] The same firms sell and build on the mainland, which puts them on the Party's platforms for revenue. Mainland China supplied 12 percent of Micron's revenue in the year before the 2025 report of its exit. \[9\]

#### Principles and Governance. What Held the Defenders Together

The defenders kept their principles. Courts demanded proof, trade law demanded findings and research stayed open. Those principles cost time, and they are worth the cost. No venue held the seven channels together, and the one program built for the purpose lasted less than three and a half years.

### 4. Gap Analysis

**The principles audit**

| Principle | The Party's design | The defenders' design |
|---|---|---|
| **Time** | Plans run on fifteen-year horizons and ten-year targets | Cases and trade reviews run on dockets and statutory cycles |
| **Entropy** | One set of plans and few handoffs | Seven owners and a handoff at every channel |
| **Topology** | A star with the plans at the hub | A mesh of firms, agencies and laboratories |
| **Asymmetry** | Absorbs what the other side publishes | Publishes worldwide and protects only where it files |
| **Agency** | Turns ordinary career choices into channels | Leaves each engineer and each firm to decide alone |
| **Resonance** | Patents, courts, capital and recruitment pointed one way | Seven instruments aimed separately at one campaign |
| **Phase** | Court in 172 days, regulator in 51 | Criminal case in more than five years |
| **Gradient** | Salaries and market access tilt each choice | Firms carry the retaliation risk alone |
| **Criticality** | Aims at memory, processors and AI infrastructure | Holds the chokepoint in tools and uses it well |
| **Coherence** | Governed by published plans under one authority | No owner of the whole |

The cells summarize documented acts. The audit itself is the framework's reading.

#### Gap 1. No Owner of the Transfer

*Architecture Governance*

**Documented.** Seven channels carried the transfer, and seven owners held the defense. The Justice Department's China Initiative came nearest to an owner of the whole. It sat inside one department, and the Department ended it after three years. The Trade Representative found in 2024 that the Party had not eliminated many of the practices its tariffs targeted. \[6\] \[11\]

**Argued.** Each owner behaved correctly inside its own remit. The patent office examined, Commerce designated, Justice prosecuted and the firm sued. Nobody held the capability map, so nobody could see seven correct actions answering one campaign on seven separate clocks. The Party never had this gap, because one authority writes its plans.

#### Gap 2. The Slowest Instrument Held the Main Line

*Architecture Principles, Phase*

**Documented.** The Entity List designation took effect on October 29, 2018, and stranded Jinhua's plant. The Justice Department unsealed the indictment and launched the China Initiative three days later. The criminal case against Jinhua ended in acquittal more than five years after that. The Party's court and regulator reached Micron in 172 days and fifty-one days. \[4\] \[5\] \[6\] \[8\] \[9\]

**Argued.** The defenders' fast instrument was administrative and their slow one was judicial. The program that framed the response put the slow one first. The Party ran its court and its regulator as administrative instruments, so both its answers landed within a year. Decide by designation where the facts support it, and keep the courts for the cases they can reach.

#### Gap 3. The Library With No Librarian

*Data Architecture. The signature gap*

Franklin built an efficient stove in 1742 and turned down a patent for it. He wrote that "as we enjoy great advantages from the inventions of others, we should be glad of an opportunity to serve others by any invention of ours." \[20\] The choice to publish rather than hoard is an American inheritance, not an oversight, and the gap that follows is its price.

**Documented.** The American Inventors Protection Act of 1999 publishes most applications eighteen months after filing. An applicant who files only in the United States may opt out. Foreign offices publish the applications of anyone who files abroad. A patent protects its owner only in the countries where it issues. The only security screen, the secrecy order, applies to single inventions judged harmful to national security. \[17\] Choate warned in 2005 that mainland firms were mining the published library. Congress moved the country to first-to-file in 2011, the change he had warned against. \[16\] \[17\]

**Argued.** The defenders' controls inspect items, and the harvest takes the aggregate. A secrecy order can stop one weapon design. Nothing watches what a million published applications teach a reader who files nothing in return. Disclosure is global and protection is territorial, so every published application with no foreign counterpart teaches the world and protects its owner only at home. The library needs no lock, because an open library is what patents exist to build. It needs a librarian who knows what the readers take.

#### Gap 4. The Firm Negotiates Alone

*Business Architecture, Economic*

**Documented.** The Trade Representative found in 2018 that the Party used joint-venture requirements and licensing approvals to pressure foreign firms into transferring technology. \[11\] Micron sued in California and saw the Party's court enjoin its products within seven months. Micron later became the first American chipmaker the Party's regulator targeted. Reuters reported in 2025 that it would leave the mainland's server-chip market. \[4\] \[9\]

**Argued.** The Party bargains as one buyer with one set of plans. The defenders send firms to the table one at a time, and each firm carries its own retaliation risk. Micron did what the defenders' architecture asked of it. It sued, it cooperated, and it absorbed the retaliation alone. The Gradient principle predicts the next firm's choice, which is silence.

#### Gap 5. The Court Reaches Only Its Own Jurisdiction

*Application Architecture*

**Documented.** A jury convicted Linwei Ding in January 2026 after he took Google's files while living and working in California. \[15\] The American trial of the Jinhua case in 2024 tried the company alone, and the court acquitted it. \[8\] The Taiwan court reached the engineers in Taiwan. \[3\] The China Initiative brought more than seventy prosecutions. Critics said it drifted toward academics inside the United States charged with failing to disclose foreign ties. \[6\]

**Argued.** Prosecution works where the defendant, the evidence and the witnesses sit inside the jurisdiction. It weakens as each of them moves abroad. A routine that selects cases by what it can reach will drift toward the people nearest at hand, and the China Initiative did. Research security belongs at the level of funding and direction, never at the level of ancestry. Designation belongs to the entities abroad that no court will reach.

### 5. Target Architecture

The target keeps the openness that makes American science what it is. It changes who owns the defense and which instrument leads.

**Gap to target**

| Gap | Target element | Owner | What it costs |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. No owner of the transfer | One technology-transfer map across the seven channels, with published thresholds | National Strategic Competition Council, convening only | A statute that outlives an election cycle, and agencies that share their agendas |
| 2. The slowest instrument held the main line | Designations approved in advance against published triggers, with prosecution in support | Commerce, with Justice and the Trade Representative | Errors that land on firms named wrongly, and retaliation against American firms |
| 3. The library with no librarian | A registry of what critical-technology applications publish without foreign protection, and shorter examination | Patent and Trademark Office, reporting to the council | Examiner salaries, and inventors who keep more as trade secrets |
| 4. The firm negotiates alone | A confidential channel for transfer demands, and a response package when the Party punishes a cooperating firm | Trade Representative and Commerce | Escalation, and firms that still prefer silence |
| 5. The court reaches only its own jurisdiction | Prosecution for people and firms inside, designation for entities outside, and joint case files with allies | Justice and Commerce, with allied prosecutors | Fewer headline cases, and allies' different rules of evidence |

**Pre-committed triggers already work.** The Entity List stranded Jinhua's plant without a trial. The Holding Foreign Companies Accountable Act of 2020 set an automatic trading ban for issuers whose auditors the inspection board could not examine. In December 2022 the board reported complete access to inspect audit firms on the mainland and in Hong Kong for the first time. \[18\]

**Designation pays for speed with error.** A designation needs no trial, which is why it is fast and why it can be wrong. The target buys that speed with published criteria and an appeal route.

**The firm should not stand alone.** A firm that helps American law enforcement and then suffers the Party's retaliation carries a cost the public imposed. A response agreed in advance tells the Party the price before it acts.

**The library stays open.** Every major patent office publishes at eighteen months, so closing the American library alone would change little. The librarian's work is smaller and cheaper. Know what the critical fields publish without foreign protection, shorten the window before grant, and make sure inventors who file only at home know they may opt out.

**What the target would not have done.** It would not have undone the transfer into UMC, and it would not have made the Jinhua case provable. It would have put the fast instrument first, shared the retaliation risk and shown the defenders what their library teaches. That claim remains an argument.

### What Technology IP Proves

Technology IP proves Asymmetry. The American system publishes to the world and protects its inventors only at home, and the Party built a method on that difference.

It proves Phase again. The Party's court and regulator landed in months, the American trial took more than five years, and the fast American instrument was the one that needed no trial.

It also proves something Hong Kong could not. The defenders win where they own the chokepoint. Tools gave Commerce a control the Party could not route around, and the Entity List stranded a five-billion-dollar plant. Every other layer ran on someone else's clock or someone else's platform.

Micron spent the money and the years the invention required. The country that built the memory chip can still decide whether the next one moves in only one direction. A courtroom will not make that decision for it.

### Field Card

**What happened.** Between 2016 and 2018, a state-funded mainland chip plant used a Taiwanese contractor and two engineers who had copied files from an American competitor to build technology it could not develop on its own. American and Taiwanese courts convicted the contractor, but a federal court acquitted the mainland plant itself in 2024. Commerce's Entity List had already stranded the plant in 2018, years before any court ruled.

**The gap.** The Data layer had no librarian for what a million published applications teach a reader who files nothing in return.

**The fix.** A registry that tracks what critical-technology fields publish without foreign patent protection, paired with a shorter examination window, would close the gap the courts never could.

**Where you could work on it.** Patent examination and research-security policy, export-control compliance at a technology firm, or trade law focused on Section 301 and Entity List designations.
