## Financial Control

*The Power of the Wallet, in Beijing and at Home*

**DOMAINS** Economic · **Financial (spine)** · Information · Psychological · Legal · Association · Data and Surveillance

**DIMENSIONS** Time · Entropy · Topology · Asymmetry · Agency · Resonance · Phase · **Gradient (signature)** · Criticality · Coherence

**STRATAGEMS** Spine S2 · S10 · S14 · S19 · S22 · S30 · S35

**GAPS** Six, one at each layer of the free system's architecture. Signature gap at the Governance layer, where the rules for the switch live in guidance and executive orders rather than in statute.

In late October 2020 Jack Ma, founder of Alibaba and of the financial giant Ant Group, criticized China's banking regulators in a speech in Shanghai. On November 3 the Shanghai Stock Exchange suspended Ant's public offering, two days before trading was due to begin. The offering would have raised more than $34 billion, the largest in history at the time. \[1\]

Ma vanished from public view for months. Regulators fined Alibaba 18.2 billion yuan on antitrust grounds in April 2021 and ordered Ant rebuilt as a financial holding company. The Financial Times reported that regulators told Alipay to hand the customer data behind its lending decisions to a new, partly state-owned credit-scoring venture. Ma gave up control of Ant in January 2023, and the central bank closed the matter with a fine of 7.123 billion yuan that July. \[1\] \[2\]

The lesson reached every entrepreneur on the mainland. No financial power there exists independent of the Party.

Money is the quietest instrument of power. An army shows itself and a law needs a vote, but money shapes behavior through incentive and fear without a visible order. That makes it the purest expression of the Gradient principle.

Napoleon proved the principle two centuries earlier without a single warship at sea. His Berlin Decree of November 1806 declared it in two sentences. "The British islands are declared in a state of blockade. All commerce and correspondence with the British islands are prohibited." \[17\] No fleet enforced it. The decree itself was the weapon, an act of exclusion that closed a continent's ports by declaration alone. Money and market access work the same way today, closing a person out of the system by decision rather than by force.

This case examines the instrument twice. The first half reads the Party's system, where the constraints are gone. The second reads the free world's, where the same levers exist behind a friendlier face. The symmetry is in the mechanism. The difference is in the accountability, and the accountability is the only thing.

### The Terms of This Case

The actor in the first half is the Party, and the people of the mainland are its first victims. The Party holds down their savings, watches their payments and gates their access.

The second half turns the lens around, and it carries a guardrail. A reader who leaves believing the two systems are the same has missed the point. The free world holds the same levers, restrained by constraints the Party never had. Those constraints are the only thing standing between the two systems. Their quiet erosion should alarm a free people more than any foreign threat.

### 1. Architecture Vision

**Documented.** The Party's central bank answers to the state. Its state banks lend mostly to state enterprises. Its payment platforms serve almost every transaction, and joint memoranda signed across dozens of agencies punish people on its blacklists. Its central bank designs the digital yuan around what it calls managed anonymity. \[3\] \[4\] \[5\]

**Argued.** The Party aims to fuse the money and the rails with the behavioral record and the law. Using one's own money then becomes a privilege granted against compliance. The full fusion does not yet run at national scale. The architecture and the intent are visible.

### 2. The Party's Baseline

#### Business Architecture. The Capabilities It Fielded

The Financial domain carried the operation, because credit is where the control begins.

**Economic.** Administered deposit rates long held household returns down, so a thrifty population's savings flowed cheaply to the state sector and favored champions. The saver subsidized the state and never voted on it. \[3\]

**Financial.** Who rises and who fails is to a large degree a political decision, mediated by Party standing and the web of connection called guanxi.

**Information.** The control arrives dressed as protection, in the language of consumer safety, financial stability and fraud prevention.

**Psychological.** The disciplined tycoon teaches compliance to everyone watching. Ma sat in the audience when Xi Jinping met private entrepreneurs in February 2025, and the rehabilitation taught the same lesson as the fall.

**Legal.** Anti-money-laundering, anti-fraud and anti-monopoly law serve as instruments that gate economic access. Capital controls limit how much an ordinary citizen may convert out of the currency each year.

**Association.** The Party seats its units inside private firms and takes special management shares in the largest platforms, as *Military-Civil Fusion* and *The Borrowed Knife* record.

**Data and Surveillance.** Two mobile platforms carry nearly all everyday payment, and the state can compel both.

#### Data Architecture. What It Knew

The payment rail is the Party's system of record for a population's life. Every transaction on the two platforms can reach the state.

The Ant episode shows the Party taking the data layer itself. The lending data that powered Ant's credit decisions went, by the Financial Times account, into a venture the state partly owns. \[2\]

The blacklists form the other half of the record. Joint memoranda from the National Development and Reform Commission, the Supreme People's Court and dozens of other agencies bar listed people from air and high-speed rail travel and from certain schools and jobs. The system is a patchwork of lists rather than the single all-seeing score of the Western caricature. \[4\]

**Stratagem 2, Besiege Wei to Rescue Zhao**, disciplines one tycoon to send the message to all.

**Stratagem 30, Make the Host and the Guest Exchange Places**, moves the Party from regulator to owner through special management shares and Party units.

**Stratagem 19, Remove the Firewood from Under the Pot**, starves a target of credit and payment access.

**Stratagem 22, Shut the Door to Catch the Thief**, seals the exits through capital controls.

**Stratagem 14, Borrow a Corpse to Resurrect the Soul**, repurposes financial law against dissent. The Henan health codes in the *COVID-19* case turned a medical record against depositors.

**Stratagem 10, Hide a Knife Behind a Smile**, offers convenience and credit with dependence and monitoring built in.

**Stratagem 35, Chain the Stratagems**, links the central bank to the rails and the blacklists to the law. The record shows each link. The single machine is the framework's reading.

#### A Target Audience Note

The mainland entrepreneur watching Jack Ma's decade fits this form, and no direct message to any individual is required.

**Documented.** Ma's Ant Group offering was suspended two days before trading, his companies were fined billions and restructured, and he reappeared at a 2025 symposium with Xi Jinping seated in the audience for other entrepreneurs to see. \[1\] \[2\]

**Argued.** A target audience analysis would read the audience as every entrepreneur of any consequence on the mainland, not Ma personally, who serves only as the visible instance the others learn from. Vulnerability runs through the same dependence any of them share, a business that needs credit, licenses and payment access the state can withdraw. The line of persuasion needs no words at all, since the treatment of one prominent case broadcasts the lesson to every reader of the news. Susceptibility runs highest among the entrepreneurs closest in scale to the example, who can measure their own exposure against his. Accessibility requires no direct contact and no individual file, only ordinary news coverage and the shared knowledge that no fortune on the mainland sits outside the Party's reach.

#### Technology Architecture. The Platform It Ran On

The digital yuan is the destination. Its designers describe managed anonymity, private for small payments and traceable whenever the state decides it matters. The state can set programmable money to expire, limit it to approved purchases and refuse it for disfavored conduct. In architecture terms the currency becomes a policy engine on the rail itself. \[5\]

Capital controls work as an egress filter. Wealth cannot easily leave the arrangement it sits inside.

#### Principles and Governance. How It Held Together

**Gradient.** The saver descends the slope the Party built, and experiences submission as prudence.

**Coherence.** One authority owns the money, the rails and the rules. A listing on one blacklist propagates across dozens of agencies by memorandum. The record documents each instrument. The single design is the framework's argument.

### 3. The Free World's Baseline

The free world holds the same levers, divided among owners and bound by constraints. Courts and legislatures hold its defense. So do regulators, a free press and the citizen's ability to leave.

#### Business Architecture. The Same Levers

**The power to create money.** Banks create new money when they lend, as the Bank of England set out in 2014. The institutions nearest the source of new money benefit first. Sound-money critics call this a hidden transfer, and mainstream economists call it the ordinary function of banking under capital rules. The framework does not settle the economics. It observes that the power is real and concentrated. \[6\]

**Inflation.** Standard public finance names the inflation tax. It erodes savings and wages and favors debtors, the largest of which is the government. No voter approves it directly.

**Who gets caught when the system breaks.** In 2008 public money rescued the institutions at the center of the failure, while households at the edge largely went without. In March 2023 regulators protected every depositor at Silicon Valley Bank, insured or not, under a systemic-risk exception. \[7\]

**The switch.** Credit scores already gate renting, borrowing and sometimes hiring. They measure creditworthiness, not loyalty, and the distinction is real. The mechanism of gating life by a score is the one the Party's blacklists use.

**The switch pulled.** The Justice Department's Operation Choke Point pressed banks from 2013 to 2017 to drop lawful but disfavored businesses. Canada froze protesters' accounts under emergency law in 2022, and its courts later ruled the invocation unreasonable. A British bank closed a prominent politician's accounts in 2023 over his views, and its parent's chief executive resigned. \[8\] \[9\] \[10\]

#### The Money That Shapes the Universities

Institutions follow their revenue without anyone giving an order, and the universities show it plainly. The record here covers funding flows only, not what any institution teaches.

**Documented.** Section 117 of the Higher Education Act requires colleges to report foreign gifts and contracts of $250,000 or more. The Education Department investigated nineteen campuses between 2019 and 2021, and the universities then reported $6.5 billion in foreign money they had not disclosed. The executive order of April 2025 notes that only about 300 of roughly 6,000 institutions report foreign money each year. It made compliance certifications material under the False Claims Act. The House voted in March 2025 to lower the reporting threshold to $50,000, and the Senate has not acted. \[11\]

**Documented.** Students from the mainland numbered about 277,000 at American colleges in 2023 and 2024, second only to students from India, and most pay full tuition. The CHIPS and Science Act bars researchers in malign foreign talent programs from federal research awards. The White House memorandum of February 2025 named university endowments among the channels of outbound investment to review. \[12\] \[13\]

**Argued.** A university whose budget leans on foreign tuition, foreign gifts and an endowment indexed to mainland listings will tend to protect those streams. It needs no instruction from anyone. The Gradient principle predicts the slope, and the disclosure data are too thin to measure it.

#### Data Architecture. What the Free World Knew

The free world's payment data sit with many banks and card networks. Governments reach them through legal process, and Canada's emergency order directed banks to hand protesters' information to the police without a warrant. The courts later found that search unreasonable. \[9\]

The foreign-funding data sit below the reporting threshold or unreported. The public cannot see most of the money that reaches the universities.

#### Application Architecture. The Routines the Free World Ran

The rules that govern the switch changed with each administration.

| Date | Owner | Routine | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2013 to 2017 | Justice Department and bank regulators | Operation Choke Point | Ended after congressional findings |
| February 2022 | Canadian government | Emergency account freezes | Ruled unreasonable in 2024 and on appeal in 2026 |
| July 2023 | A British bank | Closure of a politician's accounts | Chief executive resigned, and the regulator reviewed |
| March 2025 | Comptroller of the Currency | Stopped examining for reputation risk | The Federal Reserve followed in June |
| August 7, 2025 | President | Order against politicized debanking | Regulators moved to write rules |

Supporters called the 2025 order a restoration of fair access. Critics warned it could weaken safeguards against fraud and money laundering. \[14\]

#### Technology Architecture. The Platforms the Free World Used

The free world's rails stay distributed across many banks and networks. An executive order of January 2025 barred federal agencies from establishing a central-bank digital currency. \[15\]

In July 2025 Congress enacted the GENIUS Act for dollar stablecoins. The law requires issuers to be able to freeze or seize tokens in response to lawful orders. The programmable switch moved from the central bank to private issuers, under a lawful-order standard. \[16\]

#### Principles and Governance. What Held the Free World Together

Canada's courts heard the freezes and condemned them. The British bank's chief executive resigned. Congress investigated the bailouts in open hearings, and the inflation debate runs on every front page. None of that is available to a subject of the Party's system.

The brakes worked, and they worked through courts, resignations and elections after the harm. The rules that govern the switch mostly live in guidance and executive orders, which each administration can rewrite.

### 4. Gap Analysis

The gaps in this case are gaps in the free world's own architecture. They mark where the free system could drift toward the Party's without ever changing its name.

**The principles audit**

| Principle | The Party's design | The free world's design |
|---|---|---|
| **Time** | Built the machine across decades | Rewrites the switch's rules each administration |
| **Entropy** | One authority over money, rails and rules | Divided owners, which is a feature |
| **Topology** | Two platforms carry nearly all payment | Many banks and networks |
| **Asymmetry** | The citizen cannot see, sue or leave | The citizen can litigate and leave |
| **Agency** | Tycoons disciplined in public | Institutions follow revenue unasked |
| **Resonance** | Blacklists propagate across dozens of agencies | Lists and powers stay separate |
| **Phase** | Pulls an offering in two days | Courts rule years after a freeze |
| **Gradient** | Repressed deposit rates tilt savings to the state | Foreign money tilts institutions below the disclosure line |
| **Criticality** | Aims at the payment rail and the credit data | Programmable switches now sit with private issuers |
| **Coherence** | Governed by one authority with no brake | Governed by law with brakes that fire late |

The cells summarize documented acts. The audit itself is the framework's reading.

#### Gap 1. The Switch Has No Statute

*Architecture Governance. The signature gap*

**Documented.** Choke Point ran through informal pressure on banks. Reputation risk lived in supervisory guidance until regulators removed it in 2025. The Canadian freezes ran under an emergency order that the courts found lacked an objective standard. The 2025 debanking order is itself an executive order. \[8\] \[9\] \[14\]

**Argued.** Policy that lives in guidance works as a shadow rule set. No one audits it, and the next administration can rewrite it. A switch that can cut a person off from the financial system needs rules written in statute, with due process no guidance can remove.

#### Gap 2. Institutions Follow the Money Unasked

*Architecture Principles, Gradient*

**Documented.** Universities failed to report $6.5 billion in foreign money until investigators arrived. Mainland students form a large tuition-paying population. Endowments hold index exposure to mainland listings. \[11\] \[12\] \[13\]

**Argued.** The Party needs no agent on campus when revenue already tilts the institution. The slope runs without an order, which is why the free world must measure it.

#### Gap 3. The Money Enters Below the Data Layer

*Data Architecture*

**Documented.** The reporting threshold stands at $250,000. About 300 of 6,000 institutions report each year. The bill to lower the threshold passed the House and stalled in the Senate. \[11\]

**Argued.** The data layer sees the large gifts that institutions choose to report and misses the rest. A board cannot manage a dependence it cannot see.

#### Gap 4. Insiders Are Caught and Households Are Not

*Business Architecture, Financial*

**Documented.** The rescues of 2008 went mostly to the institutions at the center. Regulators protected every depositor at Silicon Valley Bank in 2023 through a systemic-risk exception. \[7\]

**Argued.** The free world's capability to let a large institution fail without rescuing its insiders remains weak. That is the free world's softer version of the insider allocation guanxi performs on the mainland.

#### Gap 5. The Emergency Routine Froze First and Asked Later

*Application Architecture*

**Documented.** Canada froze accounts without an objective standard in 2022, and its courts ruled against the government in 2024 and 2026. \[9\]

**Argued.** The routine fired before any standard applied and the brake fired years later. Emergency financial powers need objective standards and sunsets written in advance, as the *COVID-19* case argues.

#### Gap 6. The Programmable Switch Moved to Private Issuers

*Technology Architecture*

**Documented.** The United States barred a central-bank digital currency by executive order and required stablecoin issuers to be able to freeze tokens on lawful orders. \[15\] \[16\]

**Argued.** The capability the Party built into the digital yuan now exists on the free world's private rails, bounded by a lawful-order standard. The standard is the whole difference, and it needs the same statute, logging and audit as any other switch.

### 5. Target Architecture

The target is not a policy toward the Party. It applies the rule of law, without exception, to the power of the wallet.

**Gap to target**

| Gap | Target element | Owner | What it costs |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. The switch has no statute | Due process in statute before any account closure or freeze on non-financial grounds, with notice, reasons and appeal | Congress | Slower action against real fraud, and litigation |
| 2. Institutions follow the money unasked | Boards report their dependence on foreign tuition, gifts and indexed holdings as a risk measure | University boards, under disclosure rules | Administrative cost, and resistance from institutions |
| 3. The money enters below the data layer | A lower threshold, disclosure of the true source and a public database | Congress and the Education Department | Paperwork, and friction with foreign partners |
| 4. Insiders are caught | Resolution rules that impose losses on insiders before any public money | Congress and bank regulators | Higher funding costs for large banks |
| 5. The emergency routine froze first | Emergency financial powers with objective standards, sunsets and fast judicial review | Legislatures | Less flexibility in the first days |
| 6. The programmable switch moved | Freezes on any programmable rail only by court order, with every freeze logged and auditable | Congress and payment regulators | Slower response to theft and sanctions evasion |

**Privacy is the default.** The target treats privacy in payment as a default rather than a privilege, and meets any programmable currency with the deepest reluctance.

**What the target would not have done.** It would not have touched the Party's machine. It would have kept the free world's switches visible, divided, contestable and escapable. That claim remains an argument.

### What Financial Control Proves

Financial control proves Gradient. Money shapes behavior by tilting the ground, and the governed experience their compliance as prudence. The Party built the most complete version in history, and its first victims are the people of the mainland.

It proves the same levers exist in the free world. The power to create money and to tax by inflation is present. So is the power to rescue insiders, to gate access by a score and to freeze an account. A book that named them only in the adversary would be propaganda.

It proves where the difference lies. The free world keeps its levers visible, divided, contestable and escapable. That restraint is the entire distance between a free financial system and an unfree one, and it lives too often in guidance rather than law.

The warning is not that the Party will impose its system on the free world. The warning is that the free world could build the same system itself, one reasonable convenience at a time, and never change the name on the door.

### Field Card

**What happened.** A month before the largest public offering in history, its founder criticized China's financial regulators in a speech, and within days regulators suspended the offering. Within years they fined his companies billions, took control of the lending data behind their business, and stripped him of control of the company he built. The same government built a digital currency with a built-in expiration switch, while the free world built the same freeze capability into private stablecoins instead, bound for the first time by a standard written into statute.

**The gap.** The rules for the financial switch live in guidance and executive orders rather than in statute, the signature gap at the Governance layer, on both sides of this case.

**The fix.** Due process written into statute before any account closure or freeze on non-financial grounds would replace guidance any administration can rewrite with a rule none of them can.

**Where you could work on it.** Financial regulation and consumer-protection policy, payments and stablecoin law, or university endowment and disclosure compliance aimed at the funding-transparency gap this chapter documents.
