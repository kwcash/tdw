## A Note on What Comes Next {.unnumbered}

This book closed with a chapter for the generation that builds the fix. It closes the back matter with the same offer extended to every other reader.

The roadmap in Appendix F is not this book's property. It is a list of jobs nobody is currently doing, addressed to whichever reader has the standing to do one of them. A reader in government can raise Appendix F's Now column with the office that already holds the authority to act on it, this quarter, without waiting for a bill to pass. A reader in industry, in a university, or in a newsroom can find the gap nearest their own seat and start closing it before anyone asks them to.
