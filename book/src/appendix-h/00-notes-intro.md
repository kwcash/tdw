## Appendix H. Notes and Sourcing, by Chapter

*The Complete Record*

This appendix gathers the sourcing notes for every case chapter in Parts I through IV, in the order those chapters appear in the book. Each chapter's footnote numbers are local to that chapter alone. Chapter Hong Kong's citation 1 and Chapter Technology IP's citation 1 are different sources. The section headers below tell a reader which chapter's numbering is in view.

Within each chapter's notes, sources marked **Documented** are supported by the citation given. Passages marked as inference, argument or reading are this book's own analytical conclusions, stated as such rather than as demonstrated fact. Every classical anchor used anywhere in this book was verified against a live source before use, and no anchor is presented as a genuine quotation without that verification.

The front matter, the Prologue, Part II and Part V of this book carry their sourcing inline rather than here. Those chapters argue rather than document a single case, and their notes stay closer to the argument they support. Appendices A through D, F and G each carry their own sourcing within the appendix itself, for the same reason.
