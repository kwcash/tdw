*The count of agencies.* The first edition said seventeen intelligence agencies. The Intelligence Community has counted eighteen organizations since 2021.

*The lifecycle and the vampire model.* This edition adds both models and maps them to the security lifecycle.

*The reinstatements.* This edition adds the 2025 order's Coast Guard results and notes the absence of a Defense-wide total.

*The Phase contrast.* The first edition compared the discharge sequence with a Hong Kong operation of five domains in forty-eight hours. The *Hong Kong* case narrowed that framing, and this edition drops the comparison.

**Stated as inference, not finding.**

*The lifecycle.* The record documents each example at each stage. Reading them as one lifecycle is the framework's argument.

*The vampire model.* The six traits are an analogy for a method and an architecture. They describe no people, and the Chinese people were the method's first host.

*The node.* The FOIA record documents the exchange. Its reading as coherence beating mass is the framework's argument.

*The domestic cases.* The record documents the dates and counts. The reading of self-consumption is the framework's argument, and it takes no position on the vaccine or on the causes of the recruiting shortfall.

*The gap readings.* Every paragraph marked Argued in the Gap Analysis belongs here, and so does the principles audit.

*The target.* Every element of the target architecture is argument.

**The discipline that must survive any edit.** Case One sets aside the medical question. Case Two states its weakness first. An edit that strengthened either case by taking a side would turn a structural finding into a partisan claim and lose the readers the chapter most needs.
