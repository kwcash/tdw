**Stated as inference, not finding.**

*The coordination.* The record documents the statutes and each channel. One engine running them is the framework's argument.

*The management plane.* The record documents the commissar system, the Party units and the certificates. Reading them as an out-of-band management plane is the framework's reading.

*The banality.* Arendt's term describes Eichmann's Germany. Applying it to the Party's bureaucracy is an analogy, and the chapter claims no equivalence of crimes.

*The lineage.* The judgment that the Party stands outside Sun Yat-sen's republican lineage is normative and historical.

*The gap readings.* Every paragraph marked Argued in the Gap Analysis belongs here, and so does the principles audit.

*The target.* Every element of the target architecture is argument.

**The guardrail that must survive any edit.** The subject is a program and a legal compulsion, never a community. The chapter opens and closes on the 442nd because the thought experiment carries the argument's ethical spine.
