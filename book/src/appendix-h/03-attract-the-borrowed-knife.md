**Corrections and changes from the first edition.**

*The patent channel.* The first edition ran two channels in one case. The patent channel and Choate's paper now live in *Technology IP*, and this case carries the capital channel alone. The move also retires the old remedy to restore a publication exemption the law already provides.

*The two lists.* The first edition said the 1260H designation carried no investment restriction until 2026. The fuller record shows two lists. Treasury's list bars investment, and Defense's list binds only procurement. Gap 3 now carries that distinction.

*The voice.* The first edition spoke in the first person plural and used Beijing as the actor. This edition names the Party and the collaborators and speaks of neither as "we."

**Stated as inference, not finding.**

*The design.* The record documents the Party's wall, its approval regime and its secrecy law. That the Party used the American capital market as one designed instrument is the framework's argument.

*The plum tree.* The record documents the timing of the state-owned exits. Reading them as a deliberate sacrifice is the framework's argument.

*The slope.* The record documents the fee structure. The claim that it, rather than any agent, carried the operation is the framework's argument.

*The collaborators.* The word carries its plain sense of working on the same deals. The record documents the work and the fees. It shows no one working for the Party.

*The flywheel and the gift.* The record documents the compensation, the special management shares and the committee's findings. The flywheel and the reading of the capital as a gift in control terms are the framework's arguments.

*The gap readings.* Every paragraph marked Argued in the Gap Analysis belongs here, and so does the principles audit.

*The target.* Every element of the target architecture is argument, and every open question is a ground for inquiry rather than a finding.

**Narrowed or removed.**

The first edition compared the VIE flow to the CHIPS Act and to venture funding for American AI, and it claimed a flat return on the China benchmark from 2010 to 2024. Those comparisons had no citable basis in the chapter and are gone. The estimate that share-based compensation across the cohort passed one hundred billion dollars is gone too. The cited filings carry the point without it. The typical underwriting spread of four to seven percent is gone, because the largest offerings paid far less. The ten-dimension walk became the principles audit, and all ten dimensions survive there.
