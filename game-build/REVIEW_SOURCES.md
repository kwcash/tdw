# Brief for the independent source reviewer

Thank you for checking the sources of Total Domain War. This brief tells you what to check, how to record it and what happens next. You need a spreadsheet program and a web browser. You need no other tool.

## What the game is

Total Domain War is a strategy game that plays out how the Chinese Party-state contests other states. Each operation in the 2026 build states a record of real events and then a reading. You check the record. The reading is argued, and it is not your task.

The game tests whether a rule can be written down and played, and proves nothing by itself. The record still has to be right.

## Who can review

You did not write the game or the book it draws on, and you have no stake in either. Tell the owner about any tie to the people or the bodies named in the claims before you start. The owner keeps your name. The project's files call you Reviewer A.

## What you receive

- `worksheet.csv`, which opens in any spreadsheet program. It lists 88 claims, one to a row.
- `SOURCING_2026.md`, which says where each claim came from and why it waits for a check.
- The link to the 2026 build, so you can read each claim where players see it.

## The worksheet

| Column | What it holds | Who fills it |
|---|---|---|
| item | The claim's id, such as S-W001-1 or EV-007 | Already filled |
| where | The operation, the calendar or the evidence card | Already filled |
| claim | The claim as the game states it | Already filled. Do not change it. |
| now_rests_on | The source the game has now. GK means the author's general knowledge, with no source yet. | Already filled |
| status | Why the claim waits for a check | Already filled |
| verdict | One of the four verdicts below | You |
| reviewer_source | One https link to the source that settles the claim | You |
| reviewer_note | What you found, in a sentence or two | You |
| checked_on | The date you checked, written like 2026-11-02 | You |

## The four verdicts

| Verdict | Use it when | You must give |
|---|---|---|
| Confirmed | A reliable source states the claim as the game words it. | The source |
| Confirmed with change | A reliable source supports the claim once a detail changes, such as a date or a number. | The source and the change in the note |
| Not supported | You looked and found no reliable source, or the sources say otherwise. | A note on what you looked for |
| Cannot check | You could not reach the source, for example behind a paywall or in a language you do not read. | A note on why |

## How to check a claim

1. Read the claim in the worksheet and in the game.
2. Find the best source you can. Prefer the primary record. A law, a government statement, a court filing and a body's own report all count. Use a major news outlet when no primary record exists, and say so in the note.
3. Check the event date. Nothing after 12 October 2026 counts as record. A later source may report an earlier event, and the note should say when the source was published.
4. Check each number. When sources differ, give the most conservative figure that a reliable source supports.
5. Check the wording. An allegation must read as an allegation. The actor must be the Party, the Party-state or a named agency, never the Chinese people.
6. Fill in the four columns. Leave a row blank if you have not reached it. A worksheet can come back in parts.

## What to leave out

- Do not change the claim column. Put any correction in the note.
- Do not add a figure for Xinjiang or for detention. The game carries none, by rule.
- Do not name a private person in a note. Name an office or a public body instead.
- Do not judge the readings or the game's balance. A note on them is welcome by separate message.

## How to send it back

Save the sheet as CSV with the same name and send it to the owner. The owner passes it on. A checker then confirms that every filled row is complete and lists the claims that need a change.

## What happens next

- A confirmed claim moves to Use in SOURCING_2026.md, with your link.
- A claim confirmed with a change is corrected in the game. The owner approves each correction first.
- An unsupported claim is cut or reworded with the owner's approval.
- A claim you could not check stays marked Check, with your note.
- REVIEW_LOG.md records each round, with its date and its counts. The project's files never carry your name.

## Time

At about ten minutes a claim, the whole sheet takes about fifteen hours. The calendar rows and the evidence cards come last in the sheet. You may start with the operations, which players read most.
