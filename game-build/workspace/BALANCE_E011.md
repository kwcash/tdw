# Balance report, E011 The Manchurian Trap, 13 October 2026

E011 is a tier 4 operation tuned with `handScale` only (0.82). The tier 4 target for the scripted main player is 42 percent.

E011 is unusual because the main result depends on one choice. A player who never gives up Manchuria pays its cost every turn. Each figure below is the share of 1,000 games won on each of three seed sets, with the scripted player at the operation's own tier. The policies differ only in when they give up a consuming holding.

| Policy | Seed set 1 | Seed set 2 | Seed set 3 | Mean |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Never gives up a holding | 6.1% | 4.9% | 4.2% | 5.1% |
| Gives up on turn 1 | 43.4% | 40.5% | 36.2% | 40.0% |
| Gives up on turn 2 | 33.5% | 32.4% | 26.6% | 30.8% |
| Gives up on turn 3 | 19.0% | 18.2% | 17.2% | 18.1% |
| Gives up on turn 5 | 5.9% | 4.1% | 4.8% | 4.9% |

## Reading

- The tuning target was the turn 1 policy. Its mean of 40.0 percent sits near the tier 4 target of 42.
- Each turn of delay costs the player about 10 points of win rate, and by turn 5 the value matches never giving up.
- The operation therefore teaches one thing in play. Holding a position that costs more than it denies loses, and the loss grows with every turn the choice stays open.
- The scripted player is a probe, and a person may find other lines. The numbers show the shape of the curve.
