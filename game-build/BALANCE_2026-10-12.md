# Balance report, 13 and 14, 12 October 2026

Method. A scripted player reads the threat map, disrupts the most dangerous visible card, defends the domain under the most pressure and clears slopes. It plays each operation at the operation's own tier. Each figure below is the share of 400 games it won on seeds the tuning never used. A second, weaker player (it only detects, disrupts visible cards and defends the hottest domain) gives a floor.

The two new operations were tuned with `handScale` only. W013 uses 0.81 and W014 uses 1.07. The settings of W001 to W012 did not change.

## Reading

- **W013 The Ballot** (tier 4) wins 44% for the main player and 9% for the weak one. The tier 4 operations W006 and W007 win 43% and 39% for the main player and 1% and 17% for the weak one. W013 sits inside that range.
- **W014 The Special Budget** (tier 6) wins 19% and 1%. The tier 6 operations W011 and W012 win 17% and 17% for the main player and 3% and 0% for the weak one. W014 sits inside that range.
- The base game targets a falling curve from 97 percent at tier 1 to 18 percent at tier 6. W013 lands within two points of its tier 4 target of 42 percent. W014 lands within one point of its tier 6 target of 18 percent.
- A script check on 60 games each confirms that the scripted beats fire. W013 beats fire on turns 3 (60 of 60 games), 6 (48) and 8 (28). W014 beats fire on turns 3 (60), 5 (58) and 7 (31). The later beats fire less often because many games end first.
