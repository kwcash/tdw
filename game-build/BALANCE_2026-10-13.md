# Balance report, W015 and W016, 13 October 2026

Method. A scripted player reads the threat map, disrupts the most dangerous visible card, defends the domain under the most pressure and clears slopes. It plays each operation at the operation's own tier. Each figure in the first table is the share of 400 games it won on seeds the tuning never used. A second, weaker player (it only detects, disrupts visible cards and defends the hottest domain) gives a floor.

The two new operations were tuned with `handScale` only. W015 uses 0.65 and W016 uses 1.06. The settings of the other operations did not change.

## Larger check on the two new operations

A win rate on a steep curve moves by a few points between samples. The two new operations were therefore also run for 1,000 games on each of three further seed sets with the main player.

| Operation | Tier | Seed set 1 | Seed set 2 | Seed set 3 | Mean |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| W015 | 4 | 43% | 43.5% | 46.8% | 44.4% |
| W016 | 5 | 24.9% | 27% | 28.5% | 26.8% |

## Reading

- **W015** (tier 4) averages 44.4% for the main player over 3,000 games. The other tier 4 operations win W006 43%, W007 39%, W013 44% over 400 games.
- **W016** (tier 5) averages 26.8% for the main player over 3,000 games. The other tier 5 operations win W008 26%, W009 27%, W010 32% over 400 games.
- The base game targets a falling curve from 97 percent at tier 1 to 18 percent at tier 6. The tier 4 target is 42 percent and the tier 5 target is 28 percent.
- The first W016 setting (1.07) gave 21% on one 400-game sample. It was retuned to 1.06 on 1,000 games.
