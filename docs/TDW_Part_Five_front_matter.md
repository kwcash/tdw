# PART FIVE: The Cases

*A Note Before the Cases*

---

The apparatus is now complete. Ten dimensions, twelve domains, thirty-six stratagems. What follows is nine campaigns read through all three at once.

A word on how these chapters are built, because it explains something that will otherwise look like repetition.

Each case runs the complete framework — every dimension in the framework's order, the domains it engages, the stratagem spine — even where two or three dimensions carry most of the weight. Each names its signature dimensions at the top so you know where the load sits. Taiwan, which runs substantially the entire repertoire, carries the one complete thirty-six-stratagem mapping in the book. The rest give their spines.

**That completeness is not thoroughness for its own sake. It is scaffolding.**

A first reading can only show you the framework applied. It cannot show you the framework working, because working means recognizing a pattern before someone points it out. So the cases are written to be returned to.

On a second pass, having finished the book, you will find details in each case that the first reading had no place to put. A financing structure that now reads as Gradient. A timing choice that now reads as Phase. A concession that now reads as Agency capture, made years before anyone noticed the capture. Those details are not omissions. Many are in the text already, doing quiet work. Others are in the sources, waiting.

The framework is designed to hold more than any single account of a case can carry.

---

**Three disciplines run through every chapter that follows, and they are worth stating once here rather than defending nine times.**

**The actor is the Party, never the people.** The Chinese Communist Party is a political organization that has ruled the mainland since 1949. It is not China, and it is not the 1.4 billion people it governs. Every case in this part shows the same instrument turned inward before it was turned outward, and the population that lives under it is the population on which the method was tested first. A reader who finishes this part distrusting Chinese people has read past what the evidence supports.

**Documented fact is held apart from argued inference.** Each chapter closes with a Sourcing Status section that says plainly which is which. Where a chapter argues that separately documented activities constitute one coordinated campaign, it says that the components are documented and the coordination is argued. Where a famous figure turned out to be famous as propaganda or as marketing, it is reframed and the reframing is stated.

**Contested numbers are stated at the precision their sources support.** Several figures in these chapters are narrower than the versions in general circulation. That is deliberate. A conservative figure that survives challenge is worth more to this argument than a striking one that does not, and each narrowing is recorded with what it would take to restore a number.

---

The final test of a framework is not whether it explains the cases an author selected. It is whether it explains the case a reader finds tomorrow, in a domain this book never named, run by an actor this book never mentioned.

Read these once to see the instrument used. Read them again to use it.
