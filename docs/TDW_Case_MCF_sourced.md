# Military-Civil Fusion

*The Long Reach That Conscripts Every Company, Researcher, and Citizen*

**DOMAINS** Legal · Economic · Financial · Information · Association · Cyber · Cultural · AI/Cognitive · Biological · Data/Surveillance/Intelligence · Psychological

**DIMENSIONS** Time · Entropy · Topology · **Asymmetry (signature)** · Agency · Resonance · Phase · Gradient · Criticality · **Coherence (signature)**

**STRATAGEMS** All thirty-six. Spine: S3, S8, S14, S17, S24, S31, S35. Spine below.

---

In October 1944, in the Vosges Mountains of eastern France, the 442nd Regimental Combat Team, Americans of Japanese ancestry, fought through six days of forest to reach a Texas battalion that had been surrounded and written off. The 442nd took more casualties than the number of men it rescued.[^1] It would end the war as the most decorated unit of its size and length of service in the history of the United States Army. Many of its soldiers had volunteered out of the internment camps where their own government had imprisoned their families behind wire. They fought under a motto borrowed from gamblers' slang, *go for broke*, and they settled, past any argument an internment order could make, the question of whether they were Americans.

They were.

They could prove it because Imperial Japan could not reach them.

That is a claim about capability, not about restraint. Tokyo was not scrupulous. It ran an aggressive propaganda apparatus, it had operated consular networks in Hawaii and on the West Coast before the war, and it would have used a lever on the Nisei had one existed. None did, and the reason was technological and institutional.

Consider what coercing a Japanese American in 1942 would actually have required. Tokyo would have needed to identify which specific families in Japan corresponded to which specific individuals in Honolulu or Los Angeles, and to keep that mapping current across an ocean and a war. It would have needed a channel to deliver a threat and receive a response, in a world where transpacific civilian communication had been severed and the only wireless traffic moving was military and monitored. It would have needed some way to verify that the person had complied, since a threat that cannot check its own result is not a lever but a hope. And it would have needed a legal instrument obliging the target to obey, which no state then possessed over people living permanently under another flag.

Every one of those requirements failed. Not because Japan was decent, but because in 1942 the physics of distance, the state of communications, and the reach of law all stopped at the water. The Nisei was beyond the empire's grasp as a matter of engineering. He was therefore free to be exactly what he was, and what he was is documented: the Munson and Ringle reports, prepared for the American government before internment, both found the population overwhelmingly loyal and internment unnecessary.[^2a] The 442nd then settled the question in blood the reports had already settled on paper.

**Now run the thought experiment forward.**

Suppose Imperial Japan had possessed what the Chinese Communist Party possesses today. A statute obliging every citizen and every person of Japanese descent abroad to assist its intelligence organs on demand. A registry linking each emigrant to relatives still inside its borders. Instantaneous encrypted communication into any American city. The means to verify compliance and to punish its absence at a distance, quietly, against a mother or a brother.

Under those conditions the Nisei in Honolulu is in a wholly different position, and so is his government. Not because his loyalty has changed — it has not, and the record says it would not have — but because a hostile state now holds a lever on him that he did not ask for, cannot refuse, and cannot make public without endangering the very people the lever is attached to.

That is the situation of a great many Chinese nationals and Chinese-Americans today, and the thought experiment is the point of this chapter. **The variable that changed between 1942 and now is not the character of the people. It is the reach of the regime.**

And here the experiment delivers its hardest result. If Imperial Japan *had* held that reach, internment would still have been the wrong answer, because internment punishes the person the lever is attached to rather than cutting the lever. The correct response to a coerced population is never to treat coercion as evidence of guilt. It is to break the mechanism of coercion and to protect the people it targets — which is precisely what a free society is for, and precisely what the United States failed to do in 1942 when no mechanism even existed.

That is the entire subject of this chapter. Military-Civil Fusion is the deliberate construction of the long reach Imperial Japan never had. A legal and institutional machine built to convert a people's talent, labor, and ancestry into the instruments of a regime, whether they consent or not. The technology closed the distance. The statute supplied the compulsion. What remains is whether the country that got 1942 wrong can get this one right.

## The Terms of This Case

Begin with what the 442nd settled, because it is the guardrail this chapter cannot do without.

**Ancestry is not loyalty.** The internment of Japanese Americans was a moral catastrophe and a strategic error at once. It imprisoned the loyal, squandered their talents, and proved nothing except that a free society can frighten itself into betraying its own principles. The lesson for the present is exact. The threat from Military-Civil Fusion is never a person's Chinese ancestry, and any defense that drifts toward suspecting Chinese students, researchers, or Chinese-Americans as a class repeats the internment error and, worse, does the Chinese Communist Party's work for it. The variable was never the blood. It is, and always has been, the reach of the regime.

Consider, too, who the authentic father of modern China was. Sun Yat-sen, honored on both sides of the strait as the founder of the Chinese republic, spent his formative years in Hawaii, sent as a boy to live with his elder brother in Honolulu, where he attended Iolani School and then Oahu College, the school known today as Punahou, which would a century later educate a future American president.[^2] American democratic ideals shaped him, and when he built his political philosophy, the Three Principles of the People — national dignity, popular sovereignty, and the people's livelihood — he drew openly on Abraham Lincoln's government of the people, by the people, for the people. He loved the Chinese people and founded a republic meant to serve them. The Republic of China that governs Taiwan today, now a full democracy, is the living heir of that vision.

The Chinese Communist Party is not that heir. It is the local franchise of a European ideology, Marxism-Leninism, and by its own record it belongs to the totalitarian family of the twentieth century rather than to the republican lineage Sun founded. Its rule produced the deadliest famine in recorded history, tens of millions dead in the Great Leap Forward from policies the Party itself imposed, followed by the Cultural Revolution it unleashed upon its own people.[^3] Measured by its fruits, it stands in the company of Lenin, Stalin, and Pol Pot, not in the company of the man educated at Punahou who modeled a republic on Lincoln. This distinction is not ornament. It is the reason the Party must claim Sun's mantle even as it betrays his principles, and it is why the people it rules are better understood as its subjects than as its members.

Military-Civil Fusion is how that regime builds the long reach Imperial Japan lacked. It is a national strategy, personally overseen by Xi Jinping, whose purpose is to erase the line between China's civilian economy and its military, so that any advance in a Chinese laboratory, company, or university is immediately available to the People's Liberation Army.

And it rests on law. The 2017 National Intelligence Law states, in Article 7, that any organization or citizen must support, assist, and cooperate with state intelligence work.[^4] A companion provision authorizes the security organs to draw on the resources and equipment of any cooperating entity. No opt-out exists, no warrant the citizen can demand, no corporate firewall the law respects. Every Chinese company, every researcher, every citizen is, by statute, a potential node of the security state, including those living abroad, whose relatives at home become the lever.

Which returns the chapter to its guardrail. A Chinese graduate student in an American laboratory, legally bound to surrender what she learns if the state demands it, on pain of consequences to her family, is not the Party's agent in any sense the 442nd would recognize. She is, far more often, its conscript, coerced by the same regime the West is trying to counter. The reach that menaces the open world captures the Chinese people first. They are the earliest victims of Military-Civil Fusion, not its authors.

The defense must therefore cut the reach — the legal compulsion, the funded channels, the institutional capture — and never the people, because suspicion by ancestry is precisely the outcome the Party wants. It isolates the diaspora, confirms the Party's claim that the overseas Chinese have no home but the People's Republic, and turns free societies into the closed and suspicious places the Party insists they secretly are.

## What Military-Civil Fusion Is

MCF is best understood not as a program but as an architecture in three layers.

**The first is legal.** The 2015 National Security Law obliged all citizens and organizations to safeguard national security. The 2017 National Intelligence Law converted that obligation into an active duty to assist the security organs. The later data and cybersecurity laws extended it into the digital realm.

**The second is institutional.** Xi Jinping chairs a central commission, established in 2017, to drive fusion across every sector and dissolve the walls between commercial research and the defense industry.

**The third is operational.** A full-spectrum acquisition engine combining the licit and the illicit. State-directed investment in foreign technology firms, talent-recruitment programs led by the Thousand Talents Plan, academic partnerships, forced technology transfer as the price of market access, and a relentless campaign of cyber-enabled theft.

The Party describes the spirit of the approach as picking flowers in foreign lands to make honey in China. The genius and the menace of the strategy is that it weaponizes openness. It need not breach the West's defenses when the West's laboratories, capital markets, and universities are open by design and Chinese entities are legally compelled to carry home whatever they find.

## The Domains

MCF, like the Taiwan campaign, reaches across nearly all twelve domains, because its entire purpose is to fuse the civilian and the military until no domain is exempt. Legal compulsion is the spine. The openness asymmetry is the opportunity. The rest are the channels.

### Legal: The Statute as Master Key

The Legal domain is the foundation the rest stands on. The 2015 National Security Law's Article 11 made safeguarding national security an obligation of every citizen and organization. The 2017 National Intelligence Law's Article 7 turned that into a positive duty to support, assist, and cooperate with state intelligence work, and further articles protect those who comply and authorize the security organs to use a cooperating entity's resources at will.

The effect is a legal master key. No purely private Chinese company or apolitical Chinese researcher exists, in law, where the state's security interest is concerned. This is why a nominally commercial firm cannot credibly promise a foreign partner that its technology will stay civilian. The promise is not the company's to make. The statute has already answered.

### Asymmetry: Openness as the Attack Surface

This case's signature dimension is where MCF draws its power. Western universities publish their research. Western markets welcome investment. Western laboratories accept the world's brightest students. China's equivalents do none of these things reciprocally.

The result is a one-way valve. Full access flowing in, almost none flowing back, and a legal duty on the Chinese side to convert that access into capability for the state. The open society's greatest strength, its openness, is, against an adversary built this way, its widest attack surface, and it cannot simply be closed without the host becoming something other than free.

### Economic: Dual-Use and the Hardware in the Walls

In the Economic domain, MCF embeds Chinese hardware inside foreign critical systems and harvests dual-use industry. The case of the rail cars is concrete. A Chinese state-owned manufacturer whose parent sits on the U.S. Department of Defense's list of Chinese military companies won American transit contracts on price alone, placing its cars beneath major cities through ordinary procurement that triggered no national-security review.[^5] Multiply that across ports, telecommunications, drones, and logistics, and the civilian supply chain becomes a distributed military asset, paid for, installed, and maintained by the target.

### Financial: Western Savings Funding the PLA

The Financial domain turns the West's own capital against it. Chinese firms tied to military modernization have raised money on American exchanges and sit inside the index funds that hold ordinary citizens' retirement savings, so that Western pension money quietly finances companies the West's own defense department has designated as military entities. The financial system funds the very capability the security system is trying to counter — the same destructive interference documented in the Taiwan case, here running through the savings of people who have no idea they are paying for it.

### Association: Talent and the Diaspora

The Association domain supplies the human channel. Talent-recruitment programs, led by the Thousand Talents Plan, draw established researchers toward Chinese institutions with funding and prestige, while the United Front Work Department cultivates the overseas Chinese community, professional associations, and friendly foreign elites.[^6] The coercive edge is never far. The same network that offers a grant can also remind a researcher that his parents still live within the regime's reach.

This is Agency made operational. It is also where the chapter's guardrail matters most, because the target of any countermeasure must be the program and the coercion, never the community.

### The Technology Channels

The remaining domains are the proving grounds. In the **AI/Cognitive** domain, commercial champions route advanced algorithms and talent straight to the PLA's pursuit of intelligentized warfare. In the **Biological** domain, genomics giants and the military medical academies gather genetic and biotechnology research and data at global scale. In the **Data/Surveillance** domain, the data-sharing mandates fuse commercial information into the security apparatus, and surveillance-technology firms supply both the domestic control grid and an export product. In the **Cyber** domain, intrusion and intellectual-property theft take by force whatever the legal and financial channels cannot reach. In the **Cultural** domain, exchange programs and language institutes cultivate access and goodwill inside foreign universities. And in the **Information** domain, the open scientific literature is simply read, indexed, and absorbed, the cheapest acquisition of all. The **Psychological** domain runs underneath them, in the steady pressure applied to the families of those abroad.

## The Ten-Dimension Reading

MCF is read most clearly with Asymmetry and Coherence held in view — the one-way openness it exploits, and the legal fusion that makes that exploitation total. But it engages all ten dimensions, and the reading runs in the framework's order.

**Through Time**, MCF is a patient harvest. The Thousand Talents Plan accumulated foreign expertise for some sixteen years before Western counterintelligence treated it as a coordinated program rather than a scatter of individual cases. The regime does not need this year's breakthrough. It needs a steady inflow across decades, and the decades belong to it.

**Through Entropy**, MCF runs at low internal cost while raising the West's. A single fused command identifies a target technology and directs the whole acquisition engine at it, while the West's response is spread across universities, agencies, and companies that must each be persuaded separately. The acquirer expends little order. The defender burns enormous energy merely cataloguing the problem.

**Through Topology**, MCF is the star pulling every civilian node toward the military center. Where a free economy keeps its companies, universities, and laboratories as independent nodes, the fusion strategy wires them all back to a single hub, so that a discovery anywhere in the Chinese system is, in principle, one hop from the PLA. The West's mesh has no such pathway. Its discoveries do not automatically reach its military, which is a virtue for liberty and a disadvantage in this particular contest.

**Through Asymmetry**, a signature dimension here, the openness valve is the whole mechanism. The acquirer enjoys total access to the open world and grants almost none in return, and the imbalance is locked in by law. The Chinese researcher abroad operates under a duty to bring knowledge home that has no Western equivalent flowing the other way. Superior Western research is, by this asymmetry, partly research conducted on the adversary's behalf.

**Through Agency**, MCF captures the open world's own institutions to do its acquiring. A foreign laboratory that hosts the talent, a foreign exchange that lists the firm, a foreign university that prizes the collaboration. Each pursues its own legitimate interest while serving the fusion strategy, and none need know it is doing so. The most efficient acquisition is the one the target carries out itself, believing it is simply doing science or business.

**Through Resonance**, the licit and the illicit reinforce one another. Investment opens the door that talent walks through. The talent identifies what the cyber operation later takes. The academic partnership launders the relationship that makes the investment look benign. No single channel is decisive. The amplification comes from running them together at the same target.

**Through Phase**, MCF acquires faster than the West can defend. The acquisition cycle — spot, fund, extract, assimilate — runs continuously, while the defensive cycle of identifying a threat, drafting a control, and enforcing it runs in years. By the time an export control names a technology, the relevant knowledge has often already crossed.

**Through Gradient**, the inducements are slopes that pull technology downhill toward China without an order being given. The grant, the prize, the market access, the joint-venture term sheet. Each tilts the terrain so that transferring technology becomes the path of least resistance for a foreign firm or researcher pursuing its own advantage. The Party shapes the incline. The target descends it voluntarily.

**Through Criticality**, MCF concentrates on the chokepoint technologies whose capture cascades furthest. Advanced semiconductors, artificial intelligence, quantum, aerospace, and biotechnology, the priority domains where a single decisive gain reshapes the military balance. The strategy does not spread itself evenly. It positions at the tipping points.

**Through Coherence**, the second signature and the universal master, MCF is the erasure of the very lines the West relies on. The free world's defense assumes a boundary between civilian and military, private and state, commercial and security. The fusion strategy abolishes those boundaries by law, so that the West is defending a line the adversary no longer recognizes. That is the deepest advantage of all. Not that the Party coordinates its civilian and military sectors, but that it has dissolved the distinction the West's entire protective architecture is built upon.

MCF runs a recognizable spine of the stratagems.

**Stratagem 24, Borrow the Road to Conquer Guo**, is its signature. Civilian collaboration and market access are the road through which military capability quietly passes.

**Stratagem 8, Openly Repair the Gallery Roads, March Secretly Through Chencang**, runs an openly civilian enterprise whose output is routed to the PLA.

**Stratagem 17, Cast a Brick to Attract Jade**, offers modest grants and positions to extract research worth many times their cost.

**Stratagem 31, The Beauty Trap**, cultivates and compromises the key personnel whose cooperation brings a laboratory's knowledge with them.

And **Stratagem 35, Chain the Stratagems**, combines investment, talent, partnership, coercion, and theft into one coordinated engine.

## What Military-Civil Fusion Proves

MCF proves that the openness asymmetry is the open world's deepest structural vulnerability. A free society's openness is its attack surface, and a regime that has fused its civilian and military sectors by law can convert that openness into military capability faster than the target can screen it. It proves, too, the depth of the Coherence advantage. The boundary the West relies on, between civilian and military, private and state, is exactly the boundary the Party has abolished, so that the West is defending a line its adversary no longer recognizes.

But the deepest thing MCF proves is who it conscripts first. The Chinese researcher, the nominally private founder, the student abroad. Each is bound by a law none of them voted for and pressured through family none of them can protect. Military-Civil Fusion is the regime's reach turned, before it is turned on anyone else, upon the Chinese people it claims to represent. They are its raw material, not its beneficiaries. This is the same truth the Taiwan and Hong Kong cases reached from other directions. The Party wages the campaign through a people who are themselves among its victims.

Read through the framework, the counter is to sever the reach without ever touching the people. Inbound and outbound investment screening. Research-security regimes that protect sensitive work without profiling scientists by ancestry. Transparency requirements for talent programs and foreign funding. Capital-market screening so that Western savings stop financing designated military firms. And reciprocity as the organizing principle, with access conditioned on the access granted in return.

Every one of these measures aims at the legal and institutional reach of the regime. None of them aims at Chinese students or Chinese-Americans as a class. The discipline to keep it that way is not softness. It is the whole strategy, because suspicion by ancestry is the single outcome that hands the Party exactly what it wants.

Return to the thought experiment one last time, because it contains the whole argument in miniature.

In 1942 the United States interned a population against which no foreign lever existed, and a generation of Nisei answered by proving in the forests of the Vosges what free people of any ancestry will do for a country that trusts them — including a country that had not. The reach was absent and America acted as though it were total.

Today the reach exists. It is written into published law, it runs on instantaneous global communication, and it is attached to real families who can really be harmed. The temptation is therefore exactly inverted from 1942, and far more dangerous, because this time the suspicion can point to a genuine mechanism rather than to nothing at all.

The answer does not change. A lever is a fact about the regime holding it, not about the person it is attached to. Cut the lever. Shield the people. That was the right answer when the reach was imaginary, and it is the right answer now that it is real.

Military-Civil Fusion is that reach, built at last and written into law. The response is to sever it, and to keep faith with the people it conscripts, on both sides of the Pacific.

Readers who date the Party's departure from Sun's principles differently, or who weigh the post-1978 record differently, are disagreeing with an argument rather than a fact.
