## Notes for Whoever Edits Next

Five passages are load-bearing in ways a copyeditor working on length will not see. Each is flagged in its own chapter's Sourcing Status; collected here so they survive handoff.

**The 442nd material in Military-Civil Fusion** is the chapter's ethical spine, not a historical anecdote before the real subject. The thought experiment — what if Imperial Japan had possessed the reach the Party possesses — is the load-bearing device. It establishes that the variable between 1942 and now is the regime's reach rather than the population's character, then delivers the result that even where the reach is real, the answer is to cut the lever rather than punish the person it is attached to. Cutting either leaves the same factual claims with nothing preventing them from reading as a case for suspicion by ancestry.

**Both halves of Financial Control.** Cut the Western material and it becomes an attack on China alone, which the author himself calls propaganda. Cut the accountability paragraph and it becomes false equivalence.

**The TSMC scoping.** "Most advanced logic chips" is deliberate. Broadening it to semiconductors generally turns a true claim false.

**The Census Bureau distinction in Domain Twelve.** What the Bureau acknowledged in 2000 versus what scholars later established from the archives. That is the difference between an admission and a research finding.

**The restraint in the two domestic cases.** Case One sets aside the medical question; Case Two states its own weakness and explicitly declines the monocausal claim about recruiting. Each concerns a subject on which readers hold strong opposing views, and the structural argument works only because it does not ask them to change those views.

**One structural check before layout.** Two orphaned header blocks were found at part boundaries in the source — "THE THIRTY-SIX STRATAGEMS" between Parts Three and Four, and "PART FIVE / THE ENTERPRISE ARCHITECTURE" inside the Taiwan case. Both are removed here. The fault appeared twice, which suggests an assembly process that produced it; verify every boundary in the final build.
